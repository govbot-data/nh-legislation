BeitEnactedbytheSenateandHouseofRepresentativesinGeneralCourtconvened:
1 1 Statement of Purpose. The purpose of this act is to allow independent contractors and hiring
2 parties to voluntarily participate in portable benefit plans, providing access to certain benefits
3 withoutalteringtheclassificationofsuchworkersunderstatelaw.
4 2 New Chapter; Voluntary Portable Benefit Plans. Amend RSA by inserting after chapter 283
5 thefollowingnewchapter:
6 CHAPTER283-A
7 VOLUNTARYPORTABLEBENEFITPLANS
8 283-A:1 Definitions. Inthischapter:
9 I. "Hiring party" means any individual or business that contracts for the performance of
10 servicesbyanindependentcontractor.
11 II. "Independent contractor" means a person who qualifies as such under RSA 275:42, II, or
12 applicablecommonlawtests,whoisfreefromcontrolordirectionovertheperformanceofservices.
13 III. "Portable benefit account" means an account owned and controlled by an independent
14 contractor, who may allocate funds to pay for portable benefits such as health insurance, non-state
15 unemployment insurance, disability insurance, life insurance, or retirement benefits, or other
16 relatedexpenses.
17 IV. "Provider" means a bank, financial institution, investment management firm, or other
18 legalentity.
19 283-A:2 VoluntaryContributionsandWithholding.
20 I. A hiring party may voluntarily contribute funds to an independent contractor’s portable
21 benefitaccount.
22 II. Contributions may be made using funds withheld from compensation owed to the
23 contractor,providedthat:
24 (a) Thewithholdingisexpressedinawrittenagreement;
25 (b) Theagreementisclear,unambiguous,andprominentlydisplayedinthecontract;
26 (c) Thecontractoraffirmativelyoptsintothewithholding;and
27 (d) Thecontractormayoptoutofsuchwithholdingatanytime.
28 283-A:3 PreservationofWorkerClassification.
