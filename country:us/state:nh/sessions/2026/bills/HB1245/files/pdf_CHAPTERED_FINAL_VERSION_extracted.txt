1 220:1 Statement of Purpose. The purpose of this act is to allow independent contractors and hiring
2 parties to voluntarily participate in portable benefit plans, providing access to certain benefits without
3 alteringtheclassificationofsuchworkersunderstatelaw.
4 220:2 NewChapter;VoluntaryPortableBenefitPlans. AmendRSAbyinsertingafterchapter283the
5 followingnewchapter:
6 CHAPTER283-A
7 VOLUNTARYPORTABLEBENEFITPLANS
8 283-A:1 Definitions. Inthischapter:
9 I. "Hiring party" means any individual or business that contracts for the performance of services
10 byanindependentcontractor.
11 II. "Independent contractor" means a person who qualifies as such under RSA 275:42, II, or
12 applicablecommonlawtests,whoisfreefromcontrolordirectionovertheperformanceofservices.
13 III. "Portable benefit account" means an account owned and controlled by an independent
14 contractor, who may allocate funds to pay for portable benefits such as health insurance, non-state
15 unemployment insurance, disability insurance, life insurance, or retirement benefits, or other related
16 expenses.
17 IV. "Provider" means a bank, financial institution, investment management firm, or other legal
18 entity.
19 283-A:2 VoluntaryContributionsandWithholding.
20 I. A hiring party may voluntarily contribute funds to an independent contractor’s portable benefit
21 account.
22 II. Contributions may be made using funds withheld from compensation owed to the contractor,
23 providedthat:
24 (a) Thewithholdingisexpressedinawrittenagreement;
25 (b) Theagreementisclear,unambiguous,andprominentlydisplayedinthecontract;
26 (c) Thecontractoraffirmativelyoptsintothewithholding;and
27 (d) Thecontractormayoptoutofsuchwithholdingatanytime.
28 283-A:3 PreservationofWorkerClassification.
29 I. The following actions shall be excluded from consideration when determining a worker’s legal
30 statusaseitheranemployeeoranindependentcontractorunderRSA275,RSA282-A,oranyotherstate
31 labor,tax,orbenefitsstatute:
32 (a) Theofferof,orcontributionto,aportablebenefitaccountbyahiringparty;
