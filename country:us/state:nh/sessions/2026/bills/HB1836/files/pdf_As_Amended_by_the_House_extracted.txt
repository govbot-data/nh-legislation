FISCALIMPACT:
The bill requires the Department of Education to withhold grant funds from a public school or
district until such time as the input-based school accountability system report is submitted to
the Department. While the Department cannot predict future compliance with this report
requirement, it states that in the most recent year there were no instances of non-compliance
and therefore this bill would have had no impact. Therefore, it is assumed compliance will
continueandthisbillwillhavenofiscalimpactinFY2026orbeyond.
