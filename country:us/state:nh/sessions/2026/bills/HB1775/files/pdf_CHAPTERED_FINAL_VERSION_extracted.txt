AMENDEDANALYSIS
Thisbill:
I. Expands the purpose of RSA 374-G to include investment in natural gas, hydrogen, and nuclear
technologiesforgridreliability.
II. Authorizeselectricutilitiestoownorinvestinsuchresourcesupto10percentofpeakload.
III. Allowsraterecoveryeligibilityforthoseinvestments.
IV. Repeals certain limitations on utility-funded generation equipment and prior investment
restrictions.
