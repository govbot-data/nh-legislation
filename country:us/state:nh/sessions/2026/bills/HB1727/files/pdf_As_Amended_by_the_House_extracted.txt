ANACT relative to the inter-agency data sharing for the purpose of distributing summer

SPONSORS: Rep.Bordes,Belk.5
COMMITTEE: ExecutiveDepartmentsandAdministration
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ANALYSIS
This bill directs the department of education and department of health and human services to
enter into a memorandum of understanding to enable the department of education to share student
addresses with the department of health and human services for the purpose of administering the
federalSummerEBTprogram.
