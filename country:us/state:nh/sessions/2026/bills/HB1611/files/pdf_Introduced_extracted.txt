ANALYSIS
This bill modifies the state's authority to seize privately owned property during a declared
emergency by limiting applicable circumstances, specifying procedural requirements, mandating
documentation,andrevisingcompensationprovisions.

METHODOLOGY:
This bill makes changes to the state's authority to seize private property during a declared
emergencyandexpandscompensationtothepropertyownertoincludereplacementcosts,lossof
use,andattorney/administrativefees.
The Department of Safety indicates that this bill could lead to a significant, though
indeterminable, increase in state expenditures. By expanding the state's authority to seize
private property, the bill would require the state to compensate property owners, potentially at
substantial cost. Since each property is unique, the financial impact would vary case by case,
dependingontheassessedvalueandassociatedcompensationrequirements.
