BeitEnactedbytheSenateandHouseofRepresentativesinGeneralCourtconvened:
1 1 New Chapter; Coal Tar. Amend RSA by inserting after chapter 125-S the following new
2 chapter:
3 CHAPTER125-T
4 COALTAR
5 125-T:1 Definitions. Inthischapter:
6 I. "Coal tar" means a viscous substance obtained by the destructive distillation of coal and
7 containing levels of polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons in excess of 10,000 milligrams per kilogram.
8 "Coal tar" includes, but is not limited to, refined coal tar, high temperature coal tar and coal tar
9 pitch.
10 II. "Coaltarsealantproduct"meansasurfaceappliedsealantproductthatcontainscoaltar
11 orcoaltarpitchvolatiles.
12 III. "Polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons" means a group of compounds that are byproducts of
13 incomplete combustion, that include several carcinogens and that are designated as hazardous
14 substances under the federal Comprehensive Environmental Response, Compensation, and Liability
15 Actof1980,42UnitedStatesCode,Section9602(a).
16 125-T:2 Prohibition. BeginningJanuary1,2027,apersonmaynot:
17 I. Sell at wholesale or retail a coal tar sealant product that is labeled as containing coal tar
18 andthatisdesignedtobeappliedonadrivewayorparkingarea.
19 II. Apply on a driveway or parking area a coal tar sealant product that is labeled as
20 containingcoaltarandthatisdesignedtobeappliedonadrivewayorparkingarea.
21 125-T:3 Exemptions. A person may request an exemption from the prohibitions in RSA 125-
22 T:2 by submitting a written request to the commissioner of the department of environmental
23 services. The request shall include the reason an exemption is needed. The commissioner may
24 exempt a person from the prohibitions in RSA 125-T:2 if the commissioner determines that the
25 person is researching the effects of a coal tar sealant product on the environment or the person is
26 developing an alternative technology and the use of a coal tar sealant product is required for
27 researchordevelopment.
28 2 EffectiveDate. ThisactshalltakeeffectJanuary1,2027.
