1 1 Short title. This chapter shall be known as "Survey All Vulnerable Electric Transformers
2 Act."
3 2 Findings.
4 I. America’s electric grid is critical to modern life and faces existential threats from solar
5 weather events (coronal mass ejections—CME), geomagnetic disturbances—GMDs, and high-
6 altitudenuclearelectromagneticpulse—HEMP,allcapableofdisablingelectricpowersystems.
7 II. High voltage transformers are especially vulnerable to geomagnetically induced
8 currents—GICs—whether induced by GMDs or HEMP E3 component, entering the grid through
9 ground-connectedneutralwires.
10 III. Extra High Voltage (EHV) transformers (345 kV–765 kV) are most vulnerable and
11 difficulttoreplace,withproductionleadtimesofupto4–6years.
12 IV. GIC vulnerability is influenced by transformer characteristics, ground conductivity, and
13 themagneticfieldintensityfromGMDorE3HEMP.
14 V. Aging transformers are more susceptible to GIC due to degraded insulating oil and coil
15 condition.
16 VI. Federal and international standards highlight the importance of transformer
17 assessmentandprotectionagainstthesethreats.
18 3 New Chapter; Survey All Vulnerable Electric Transformers Act. Amend RSA by inserting

22 378-A:1 Purpose. The purpose of this chapter is to require electric utilities and electric project
23 developers to assess and report the vulnerability of high-voltage transformers to geomagnetically
24 induced currents (GICs) caused by geomagnetic disturbances (GMDs) and high altitude
25 electromagnetic pulse (HEMP), and to recommend mitigation strategies to protect critical electric
26 infrastructure.
