BeitEnactedbytheSenateandHouseofRepresentativesinGeneralCourtconvened:
1 1 New Paragraph; Unlawful Operation or Use of Small Unmanned Aircraft System. Amend
2 RSA644:23byinsertingafterparagraphVIIthefollowingnewparagraph:
3 VIII. A person is guilty of a class A misdemeanor when the person knowingly uses a small
4 unmanned aircraft system to enter onto the land or into the airspace above the land of another
5 person without permission or otherwise to commit a trespass in order to capture any type of visual
6 image, sound recording, or other physical impression of the person engaging in a private, personal,
7 or familial activity and the invasion occurs in a manner that is offensive to a reasonable person. A
8 person is guilty of a class B felony if such person knowingly transmits, publishes, broadcasts, sells,
9 offers for sale, or otherwise publicly uses any visual image, sound recording, or other physical
10 impressionobtainedinviolationofthisparagraph.
11 (a) This paragraph shall not be construed to impair or limit any otherwise lawful
12 activities of law enforcement personnel or employees of governmental agencies or other entities,
13 either public or private, who, in the course and scope of their employment, attempt to capture any
14 type of visual image, sound recording, or other physical impression of a person during an
15 investigation, surveillance, or monitoring of any conduct to obtain evidence of suspected illegal
16 activity or other misconduct, the suspected violation of any administrative rule or regulation,
17 suspected fraudulent conduct, or any activity involving a violation of law or business practices or
18 conductofpublicofficialsadverselyaffectingthepublicwelfare,health,orsafety.
19 (b) For the purposes of this paragraph, “private, personal, and familial activity”
20 includes,butisnotlimitedto:
21 (1) Intimate details of the person's personal life under circumstances in which the
22 personhasareasonableexpectationofprivacy.
23 (2) Interactionswiththeperson'sfamilyorsignificantothersundercircumstancesin
24 whichthepersonhasareasonableexpectationofprivacy.
25 (3) Any activity that occurs on a residential property under circumstances in which
26 thepersonhasareasonableexpectationofprivacy.
27 (4) Other aspects of the person’s private affairs or concerns under circumstances in
28 whichthepersonhasareasonableexpectationofprivacy.
29 2 EffectiveDate. ThisactshalltakeeffectJanuary1,2027.
