AMENDEDANALYSIS
This bill establishes a developmental services oversight commission, revises reporting and
investigation requirements regarding reports of abuse of vulnerable adults, and revises notification and
reportingrequirementsregardingabuseofminorsininstitutionalresidentialsettings.
