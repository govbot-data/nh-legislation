AMENDEDANALYSIS
This constitutional amendment concurrent resolution would amend the constitution to recognize
the right of all United States citizens who are 18 years or older to vote in the place they claim as
domicile,solongastheyactuallyresideintheplacetheyclaimasdomicile.
