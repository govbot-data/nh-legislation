METHODOLOGY:
This bill standardizes and expands the mandatory report to voters for school district budget
meetings, including 10-year trends on per-pupil costs, teacher salaries, and average
administrator salaries, and a table of the highest-paid administrators, and revises posting and
annualwarrantrequirements. Costs,ifany,fordistrictstocomplyareindeterminable.
