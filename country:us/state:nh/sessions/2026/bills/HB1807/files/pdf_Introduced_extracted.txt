METHODOLOGY:
This bill seeks to modify the requirements relative to information posted at public budget
hearings, posted warrant articles, and ballot for elected school officials. The Department of
Education states this bill has some technical flaws which may make it difficult for school
districtstoimplementproperly.Anyimpactthisbillmayhaveonlocalschooldistrictsbudgetsis
indeterminable.
TheDepartmenthasprovidedthefollowing:
 Average Daily Membership in Residence (ADM-R) cannot be measured as of October 1st.
ADM-Rmeasuresentireschoolyearandnotamomentintime.
o Forexample,ADM-Rcouldbecapturedforthemostcurrentcompletedschoolyear.
 The definition of ADM-R, for the purpose of adequacy, only describes what the State funds
through the adequacy formula and does not describe all of the students the district is
responsible for educating through the adoption of their budget and expenditure of
appropriations.
o For example, a district may elect to have a pre-kindergarten program which is not
funded by adequacy. Furthermore, districts educate students and raise and
appropriate funds to educate students in their Average Daily Membership in
Attendance(ADM-A)thatarenotintheirADM-R.

 The bill suggests anAuthorized Regional Enrollment Area (AREA) school as compromising a
whole school district. A school district may operate a school and also may be part of an
AREAschoolagreement.
 The bill does not specify when in the apportionment process costs would be allocated, nor
doesitclarifywhichmethodologieswouldapplyincalculatingaper-pupilcostforcooperative
schooldistricts.
