METHODOLOGY:
This bill updates the required mandatory report to voters for school district budget meetings by
standardizing its format and expanding financial disclosures, including 10-year trends on per-
pupil costs, teacher salaries, and administrator pay, along with a table of the highest-paid
administrators. It also revises posting requirements and requires certain fiscal information to
appear directly in the annual school warrant. Costs, if any, for districts to comply with this bill
areindeterminable.
