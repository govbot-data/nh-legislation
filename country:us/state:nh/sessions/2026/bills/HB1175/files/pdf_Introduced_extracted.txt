ANALYSIS
This bill updates the offense prohibiting the use of a scanning device or reencorder to defraud by
including the scanning and reencoding of information contained on integrated circuit chips of
paymentcardsinsteadofjustmagneticstripsorstripes.

3 I. Apersonisguiltyofthecrimeofusingascanningdeviceorreencodertodefraudwhenthe

5 (a) Uses a scanning device to access, read, obtain, memorize, or store, temporarily or
6 permanently, information encoded on the magnetic strip or stripe or on an integrated circuit
7 chip of a payment card without the permission of the authorized user of the payment card and with
8 the intent to defraud the authorized user, the issuer of the authorized user's payment card, or a
9 merchant;or
10 (b) Uses a reencoder to place information encoded on the magnetic strip or stripe or
11 integrated circuit chip of a payment card onto the magnetic strip or stripe or integrated circuit
12 chip of a different card without the permission of the authorized user of the card from which the
13 information is being reencoded and with the intent to defraud the authorized user, the issuer of the
14 authorizeduser'spaymentcard,oramerchant.
