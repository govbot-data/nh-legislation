METHODOLOGY:
This bill repeals Chapter 141:431, Laws of 2025, a section in HB 2 (the budget trailer bill) that
appropriated$7.5millionineachyearoftheFY26/27bienniumtotheDepartmentofHealthand
Human Services for the purpose of providing grants to NH childcare providers for employee
recruitment and retention. The appropriation was to be made from federal Temporary
Assistance to Needy Families (TANF) reserve funds, and was contingent upon the Department
seeking formal approval from the federal government for this use of the funds. The Department
has since relayed to the Office of the Legislative Budget Assistant that it has sought guidance
from the federal government, which has determined that federal TANF funds cannot be used for
this purpose. For this reason, although the repeal of this section would remove the
appropriation (as well as the Department's authority to provide grants to eligible childcare
providers),therepealisanticipatedtohavenofiscalimpactinpractice.
