HOUSEBILL
ANACT requiring health care providers to disclose to patients indirect financial incentives
receivedbytheprovider.
SPONSORS: Rep.Wherry,Hills.13;Rep.Litchfield,Rock.32;Rep.McGrath,Rock.40
COMMITTEE: Health,HumanServicesandElderlyAffairs
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ANALYSIS
This bill requires a physician who receives compensation exceeding $5,000 or more as the result
of a preferential promotion to disclose this information to the patient prior to prescribing any
treatmentassociatedwiththesourceofthepromotion.
