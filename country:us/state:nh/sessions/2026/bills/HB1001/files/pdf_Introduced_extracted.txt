STATEOFNEWHAMPSHIRE
IntheYearofOurLordTwoThousandTwenty-Six
ANACT allowing for appointment by county attorneys of investigators with law
enforcementpowers.
BeitEnactedbytheSenateandHouseofRepresentativesinGeneralCourtconvened:
1 1 New Section; County Attorneys; Appointment of Investigators. Amend RSA 7 by inserting
2 aftersection33-gthefollowingnewsection:
3 7:33-h AppointmentofInvestigatorswithLawEnforcementAuthority.
4 I. Subject to available county appropriations, and with the approval of the county
5 commissioners, a county attorney may appoint investigators within the county attorney's office to
6 which he or she is elected, each of whom shall have statewide law enforcement authority and shall
7 be a peace officer as defined by RSA 594:1, III. Any person appointed to such a position shall be
8 certified or eligible for certification as a police officer pursuant to RSA 106-L:5, V. An investigator
9 appointed under this paragraph shall be removed if he or she fails to achieve certification or if he or
10 sheisdecertifiedbythepolicestandardsandtrainingcouncil.
11 II. Each appointee under paragraph I shall report to the county attorney. Operational
12 controloversuchinvestigatorsshallrestsolelywiththecountyattorney.
13 III. Nothing in this section shall preclude county attorneys from appointing investigators
14 who are not certified law enforcement officers and do not have the powers and authority of a law
15 enforcementofficer.
16 2 EffectiveDate. Thisactshalltakeeffect60daysafteritspassage.
