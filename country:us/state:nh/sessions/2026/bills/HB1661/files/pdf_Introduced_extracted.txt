METHODOLOGY:
Thisbillappropriates$750,000inFY2027and$1,500,000inFY2028andannuallythereafterto
the Housing Finance Authority to support the expansion of its community heroes initiative. The
Housing Finance Authority shall not retain more than 10 percent of the appropriation for
administrativecosts. Italsorequiresanincreaseinbothitsgrantamountandmaximumincome
eligibility limit, and introduces a new provision allowing recipients to apply grant funds toward
interestratereductions.
