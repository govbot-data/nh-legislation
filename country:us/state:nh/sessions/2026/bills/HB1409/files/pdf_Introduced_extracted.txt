METHODOLOGY:
This bill modifies the revenue distribution of video lottery terminals, after other statutory
transfers (i.e., Governor’s Commission on Addiction, Treatment and Prevention and charitable
organizations), from 75% general fund and 25% education trust fund to 0% general fund and
100%educationtrustfund(ETF). ThisbillwilldecreasegeneralfundrevenueandincreaseETF
revenue. Budget estimates (June 2025) for the general fund portion of VLT revenue are
$45,100,000inFY2026and$93,900,000inFY2027(estimatesforFY2028andbeyondwerenot
made). Since this bill is effective "upon passage", the change would likely occur sometime in the
endofFY2026orbeginningofFY2027,ifitweretobecomelaw.
