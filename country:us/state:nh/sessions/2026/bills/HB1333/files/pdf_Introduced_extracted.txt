ANALYSIS
Thisbill:
I. Allows a first-degree murder charge to be brought against a person who administers a
medication intended to terminate a pregnancy to a pregnant woman without the knowledge and
consentofapregnantwoman.
II. Amends when a homicide charge can be brought when the fetus is a victim from 20 weeks to
conception,orinthecaseofinvitrofertilization,implantation.
