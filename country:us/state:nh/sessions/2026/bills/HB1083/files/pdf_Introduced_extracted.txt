METHODOLOGY:
This bill requires any person or entity making independent expenditures exceeding $500,000 in
support of or opposition to a candidate for governor, or more than $50,000 in aggregate for
candidates for executive council, state senate, or state representative to file an itemized
statementwiththeSecretaryofState.
The Department of State indicates updates would be necessary to the electronic campaign
finance reporting system to accommodate the new disclosure requirements. The estimated cost
forthesesystemmodificationsis$50,000.
