ANALYSIS
This bill extends the amount of time insurance carriers, self-insurers, or payors acting on behalf
of an insurance carrier or self-insurer have to pay uncontested claims, and establishes an optional
mediation process where claims are contested. The bill also modifies the reasonable effort standard
for resolving claims with a good faith standard, and increases civil penalties for failure to engage in
goodfaitheffortsrelativetopaymentofclaims.
