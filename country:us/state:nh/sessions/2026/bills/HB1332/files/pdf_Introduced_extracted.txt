STATEOFNEWHAMPSHIRE
IntheYearofOurLordTwoThousandTwenty-Six
ANACT relativetoGoldStarMother'sDay.
BeitEnactedbytheSenateandHouseofRepresentativesinGeneralCourtconvened:
1 1 Gold Star Mother's and Family's Day. RSA 4:13-h, II is repealed and reenacted to read as
2 follows:
3 II. The Honor and Remember Flag, created by the national organization Honor and
4 Remember Inc., is hereby recognized as a New Hampshire symbol honoring the service and sacrifice
5 of New Hampshire's men and women who served in the United States Armed Forces and gave their
6 lives while serving or as a result of serving, as well as their families. The governor shall order the
7 Honor and Remember Flag to be displayed daily at the New Hampshire state veterans cemetery in
8 Boscawen and at the State House in the most junior order of precedence on the last Sunday in
9 September.
10 2 EffectiveDate. Thisactshalltakeeffect60daysafteritspassage.
