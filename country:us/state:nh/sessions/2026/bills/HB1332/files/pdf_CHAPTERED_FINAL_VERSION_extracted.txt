AMENDEDANALYSIS
This bill limits the flags which may be displayed over the state house to only those flags officially
recognized by the federal government by law, executive order, or an official agency regulation, with the
exceptionoftheGoldStarFlag,whichthegovernormayauthorizetodisplayonstatehousegrounds.
