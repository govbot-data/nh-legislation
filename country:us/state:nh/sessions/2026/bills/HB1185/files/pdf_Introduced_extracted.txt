ANALYSIS
This bill redefines the crime of endangering of the welfare of a child or incompetent, and adds
revisedpenaltiesfortheviolationthereof.

BeitEnactedbytheSenateandHouseofRepresentativesinGeneralCourtconvened:
1 1 Endangering the Welfare of a Child or Incompetent. RSA 639:3 is repealed and reenacted to
2 readasfollows:
3 639:3 EndangeringWelfareofaChildorIncompetent.
4 I. Any person who owes a child under 18 years of age, or an incompetent, a duty of care,
5 protection, or support, and violates all or any portion of those duties, or induces such child or
6 incompetent to engage in conduct that endangers the safety or health of such child or incompetent,
7 by engaging in conduct or a pattern of conduct so as to expose the child or incompetent to a
8 substantial and unjustifiable risk of serious bodily injury or death, shall be guilty of a class A
9 misdemeanorifsuchendangermentwasnegligent,andaclassBfelonyifknowing.
10 II. Any person convicted of a second or subsequent offense under this section shall be guilty
11 ofaclassBfelony.
12 III. If a person violates paragraph I by negligently engaging in conduct or a pattern of
13 conduct that endangers the safety or health of such child or incompetent, and as a result of such
14 exposure,causesseriousbodilyinjuryordeathofthechildorincompetent,thepersonshallbeguilty
15 ofaclassAfelony.
16 2 EffectiveDate. ThisactshalltakeeffectJanuary1,2027.
