3 32:27 ProcedureforFiscalYearChange.
4 I. Upon an affirmative vote of a majority of county voters, a county may move the biennial
5 budget to the off year of the state election. Putting the question on the ballot shall require a vote in
6 the affirmative by a majority of the county commissioners. Said vote shall happen no later than the
7 September1beforethestateelections.
8 II. Notice of a vote of the county commissioners pursuant to paragraph I shall be given at
9 least 15 days, but not more than 60 days, prior, by a local newspaper of general circulation, public
10 notice on the county's main website or any social media accounts utilized by the county, and public
11 noticepostedinthe2placeswherethecountyregularlypostsnoticesofitsgoverningbodymeetings,
12 orbyanyothermeansdeemedappropriatebythecountycommissioners.
13 III. The question shall be put to the voters at the November state election and shall read as
14 follows:
15 "ShallNAMEOFCOUNTYmovetoabiennialbudgetcyclestartingintheyearYEARNUMBER?"
16 2 EffectiveDate. Thisactshalltakeeffect60daysafteritspassage.
