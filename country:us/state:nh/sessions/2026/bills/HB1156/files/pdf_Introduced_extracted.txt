METHODOLOGY:
This bill requires estate administrators to notify local officials of anyone inheriting real estate
and certify this to the probate court, before filing a final account. The notice must also be
recordedatthecountyregistryofdeeds,whichmaychargeafee.
The New Hampshire Association of Counties states that this would generate revenue for
counties through a recording fee of $12 for the first page, plus $4 for each additional page, as
well as applicable postage. However, the total revenue is indeterminable as it depends on the
numberofprobatecasesinvolvingrealestateinheritance.
