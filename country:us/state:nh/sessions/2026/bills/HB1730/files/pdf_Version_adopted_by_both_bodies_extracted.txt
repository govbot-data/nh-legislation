AMENDEDANALYSIS
This bill reclassifies as felonies certain sexual assault conduct related to engaging in sexual
contact or penetration with a person over whom the defendant had certain authority by virtue of
their role in a correctional institution, the secure psychiatric unit, or a juvenile detention facility, or
theirroleasanadultorjuvenileprobationorparoleofficer.
