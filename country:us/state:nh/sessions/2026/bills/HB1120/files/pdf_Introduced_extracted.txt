ANALYSIS
This bill allows the adoption of subdivision regulations that require water supply studies,
determination of adequate water quantity for reasonably anticipated future water uses, and
minimum on-lot private well testing requirements for subdivisions of 4 or more units. This bill also
clarifiestheapplicant'sdutytopayassociatedcosts.
