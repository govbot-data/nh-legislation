AMENDEDANALYSIS
Thisbillmodifiestheproceduresforschoolbusdrivercriminalhistoryrecordschecksbyextendingits
application to volunteers and contractors and specifying requirements for such record checks, including
fingerprinting. The bill also directs the department of education to implement a secure system for such
record checks and authorizes the department to make rules relative to the rights of personnel in criminal
historyrecordchecks.
