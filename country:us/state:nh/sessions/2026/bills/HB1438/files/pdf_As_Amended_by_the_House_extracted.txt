BeitEnactedbytheSenateandHouseofRepresentativesinGeneralCourtconvened:
1 1 New Section; Duty to Report Animal Abuse. Amend RSA 330-A by inserting after section 35
2 thefollowingnewsection:
3 330-A:36 ReportsofAnimalAbuse.
4 I. A person licensed under this chapter having knowledge of, or suspecting in good faith,
5 that any animal has been subjected to cruelty, neglect, exploitation, or is living in hazardous
6 conditions through information from a client that would normally be protected confidential
7 information may report, or cause a report to be made, to a law enforcement officer or to the field
8 servicesofficewiththeNewHampshireSocietyforthePreventionofCrueltytoAnimals.
9 II. The duty may be discharged by, and no monetary liability or cause of action shall arise
10 against, any person licensed under this chapter if the licensee makes reasonable efforts to notify the
11 police department closest to the suspected location of animal cruelty, neglect, or exploitation, or to
12 theNewHampshireSocietyforthePreventionofCrueltytoAnimals.
13 III. No monetary liability and no cause of action may arise concerning client privacy or
14 confidentiality against any person licensed under this chapter for information disclosed to third
15 partiesinanefforttodischargeadutyunderparagraphsIandII.
16 2 EffectiveDate. Thisactshalltakeeffect60daysafteritspassage.
