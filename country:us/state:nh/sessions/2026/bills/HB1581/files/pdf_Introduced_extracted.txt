ANACT requiring municipalities to give notice to property owners when the assessed
valueoftheirpropertychanges.
SPONSORS: Rep. Wherry, Hills. 13; Rep. Ford, Rock. 3; Rep. McFarlane, Graf. 18; Rep.
McGrath,Rock.40;Rep.MaryMurphy,Hills.27;Rep.Pauer,Hills.36
COMMITTEE: MunicipalandCountyGovernment
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ANALYSIS
This bill requires municipalities to send a general notice of any change in a property's assessed
valuation to the property owner within 14 days of recording the change, unless the adjustment is
partofamunicipality-widerevaluation.
