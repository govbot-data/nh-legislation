ANACT permitting public employees to bargain individually with public employers
withoutanyintervention.
SPONSORS: Rep. Labrie, Hills. 2; Rep. Drago, Rock. 4; Rep. Drew, Hills. 19; Rep. Farrington,

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ANALYSIS
This bill provides certain public employees with independent bargaining rights and authorizes
said employees to engage in independent bargaining with employers free of any interference. The
bill also establishes penalties for any violations of these public employee's independent bargaining
rights.
