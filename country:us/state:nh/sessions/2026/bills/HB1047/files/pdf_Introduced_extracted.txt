ANALYSIS
This bill makes it a capital murder offense to knowingly cause the death of the governor or
governor-elect of New Hampshire, a member or member-elect of the executive council, or any
member or member-elect of theNew Hampshire senate or New Hampshire house of representatives,
or any candidate for any such offices after such candidate has been nominated at his or her party's
primary,whensuchkillingismotivatedbyknowledgeoftheforegoingcapacityofthevictim.
