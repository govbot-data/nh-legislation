AMENDEDANALYSIS
This bill adds definitions for mining-related terms, authorizes rulemaking for permitting,
reclamation, and fees based on mineral volume, ceases disturbance of human remains encountered
during permitted mining or prospecting activities, and establishes a transition period for pending
applicationsunderthecurrentlaw.
