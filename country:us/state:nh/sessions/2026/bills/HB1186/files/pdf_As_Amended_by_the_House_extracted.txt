AMENDEDANALYSIS
This bill recodifies the subdivision relative to the sale of eggs to reflect modern methods of
sorting,labeling,andgradingeggs. Thisbillalsorequireseggproducerswhosellfromtheirhomeor
farmstand to label their eggs and exempts such egg producers from requirements for sorting, selling
andgradingeggs.
