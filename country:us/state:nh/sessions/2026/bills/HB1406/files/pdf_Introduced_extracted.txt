BeitEnactedbytheSenateandHouseofRepresentativesinGeneralCourtconvened:
1 1 New Section; Managed Care Law; Use of Artificial Intelligence to Change the Clinical
2 Judgment of the Provider Prohibited. Amend RSA 420-J by inserting after section 6-e the following
3 newsection:
4 420-J:6-f Use of Artificial Intelligence by Health Carriers to Change Provider Codes Prohibited.
5 Health carriers are prohibited from using artificial intelligence, as defined in RSA 5-D:1, to conduct
6 audits of provider codes or to adjust such codes based on recommendations from artificial
7 intelligence that would change, alter, or amend the clinical judgment of a provider. Each carrier
8 shall maintain records identifying the use of artificial intelligence tools in claims processing and
9 make such records available to the insurance department upon audit. A violation of this section
10 shall constitute an unfair insurance practice under RSA 417, and the commissioner may impose
11 administrativefinesororderrestitutionforanydelayordenialofcareresultingfromsuchviolation.
12 2 EffectiveDate. ThisactshalltakeeffectJanuary1,2027.
