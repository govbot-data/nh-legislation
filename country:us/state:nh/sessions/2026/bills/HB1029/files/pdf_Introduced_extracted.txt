ANALYSIS
This bill changes the definition of "excavate" to clarify that hand planting of flowers, small
shrubs and bushes not exceeding 3-gallon pots, and shallow irrigation repairs dug by hand, are not
consideredexcavationforpurposesofundergroundutilitydamagepreventionlaws.
