4 I. Apersoncommits anoffense if suchperson, whenapplyingfor residential housinginNew

6 notlawfullypresentintheUnitedStates.
7 II. For purposes of this section, a person shall be regarded as lawfully present in the United

9 United States Code, section 1373(c). No landlord or property manager shall attempt to
10 independentlydetermineanapplicant'simmigrationstatus.
11 III. An applicant shall be presumed to be lawfully present if they present one of the
12 followingformsofidentification:
13 (a) Avalid,unexpiredNewHampshiredriver'slicenseornondriveridentificationcard.

15 biometricidentifier.
16 (c) A valid federal or state government-issued identification bearing a photograph or
17 biometricidentifier,issuedbyanentitythatrequiresproofoflawfulpresence.
18 (d) A foreign passport with an unexpired United States visa and a corresponding
19 DepartmentofHomelandSecurityadmissionstamp.
20 (e) A foreign passport from a visa waiver country with a valid entry stamp and
