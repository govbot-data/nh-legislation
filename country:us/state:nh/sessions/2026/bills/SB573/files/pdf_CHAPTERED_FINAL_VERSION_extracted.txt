5 167-E:1 Definitions. Inthischapter:
6 I. "Certified public safety comfort dog" means a specifically trained dog that provides support,
7 comfort, and crisis response to individuals, groups, and communities in various settings, such as
8 hospitals, nursing homes, schools, disaster relief areas, emergency response/public safety agencies and
9 other places where people may be experiencing stress, trauma, or crisis. Certified public safety comfort
10 dogsaretrainedtointeractgentlyandempatheticallywithpeople,offeringunconditionalsupportandnon-
11 judgmental companionship to help alleviate anxiety, reduce stress, improve mood, and enhance overall
12 well-being and resilience. Certified public safety comfort dogs are not considered service dogs or
13 emotionalsupportanimals.
14 II. "Certified public safety comfort dog handler" means an emergency response or public safety
15 worker who is responsible for the care, training, and supervision of a certified public safety comfort dog
16 during its interactions with individuals, groups, and communities in need of support or crisis response. A
17 certified public safety comfort dog handler shall ensure that it behaves appropriately, follows commands,
18 and maintains a calm and comforting presence in various environments. A certified public safety comfort
19 dog handler shall be trained to understand canine behavior, communication, and emotional support
20 techniques, allowing them to effectively guide the certified public safety comfort dog's interactions with
21 individuals,groups,orcommunitiesindistressorcrisissituations.
22 III. "Emergency response or public safety worker" means any law enforcement officer certified
23 under RSA 106-L, certified county corrections officer, sheriff or deputy sheriff, state police officer, civilian
24 lawenforcementemployee,civiliancountycorrectionsemployee,anycall,volunteer,orregularfirefighter,
25 civilian fire department employee, rescue or ambulance worker, including ambulance service, emergency
26 medical personnel, first responder service, and volunteer personnel, telecommunicators, and local
27 dispatchers,includinganyretiredemergencyresponseorpublicsafetyworkersasdefinedinthissection.
28 IV. "Local dispatcher" means a person who determines the location, status, and assistance
29 requiredbycallersandwalk-incustomersforpublicsafetyservicesanddispatchestheappropriatepolice,
30 fire, ambulance, or other units to provide needed emergency services at the state, city, town, or private
31 emergencyserviceslevel.
