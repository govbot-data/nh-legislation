─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ANALYSIS
This bill authorizes commissioners or heads of state agencies to apply for an exception to the
restricted uses for which artificial intelligence may be used by state agencies. This bill also directs
the commissioner of the department of information technology to create a procedure for processing
and accepting requested exceptions, with the final decision on approval being made by the executive
council.
