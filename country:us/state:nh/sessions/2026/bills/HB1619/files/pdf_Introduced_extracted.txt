3 CHAPTER674-A
4 PROPERTYOWNERS'RIGHTSANDLANDUSEPROTECTIONS
5 674-A:1 Purpose. The purpose of this chapter is to affirm and protect the fundamental rights of
6 property owners to own, use, and dispose of their property free from excessive government
7 intervention, consistent with the principles of a free market and the limited-government framework
8 ofNewHampshirelaw.
9 674-A:2 Definitions. Inthischapter:
10 I. “Property owner” means a person or entity holding legal title to real property in New
11 Hampshire.
12 II. “Municipality” means a city, town, or other political subdivision recognized by New
13 Hampshirelaw.
14 674-A:3 PropertyOwners’Rights.
15 I. Property owners shall have the right to use their property as they see fit, provided such
16 use does not infringe upon the rights of others or violate clearly defined public safety standards set
17 forthinNewHampshirelaw,includingRSA155-Aandotherapplicablestatutes.
18 II. Property owners may set rental prices based on market demand, free from government-
19 imposedrentcontrols.
20 III. Municipal zoning shall be limited to the minimum necessary to protect public health,
21 safety,andwelfare,andregulationsshallnotundulyrestrictpropertydevelopmentorusage.
22 IV. Property owners may develop their land for residential, commercial, or mixed-use
23 purposes,subjectonlytotransparentandpredictablemunicipalregulations.
24 V. No property shall be taken by the government for private use or economic development,
25 except in cases of clear public necessity. Owners are entitled to just compensation and due process
26 inanyeminentdomainproceeding.
27 VI. Property owners may enter into contracts, leases, and sales agreements without
28 governmentinterference,exceptincasesofprovenfraudorcoercion.
29 VII. Municipalities shall process property development permits in a timely and transparent
30 manner. Delaysorarbitrarydenialsviolatetheowner’srighttoproductiveuse.
