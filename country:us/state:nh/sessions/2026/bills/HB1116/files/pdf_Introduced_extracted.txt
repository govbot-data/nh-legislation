ANALYSIS
Thisbill:
I. States that a New Hampshire state judge may not claim judicial privilege to refuse to testify
inacriminalproceedingwheretheyhavefirsthandknowledgeofmaterialfacts.
II. Requires the court in small claims litigations to send notice of the claim within 10 days of its
filing.
