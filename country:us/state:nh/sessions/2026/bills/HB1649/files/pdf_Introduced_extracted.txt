BeitEnactedbytheSenateandHouseofRepresentativesinGeneralCourtconvened:
1 1 New Section; Towns, Cities, Village Districts, and Unincorporated Places; Powers and Duties
2 of Towns; Prohibition on Certain Donations. Amend RSA 31 by inserting after section 9-d the
3 followingnewsection:
4 31:9-e ProhibitiononCertainDonations.
5 I. No city, town, village district, or unincorporated place shall appropriate or expend any
6 taxpayer funds, whether through a budget or warrant article, to donate such funds to any non-profit
7 organization. This prohibition shall apply to all taxpayer funds, including general funds, reserve
8 funds,andallothertypesofpublicfunds.
9 II. The prohibition under paragraph I shall not apply to funds explicitly allocated to provide
10 grants or financial assistance to non-profit organizations through a competitive bidding process,
11 provided that such grants or assistance serve a clear public purpose and are in compliance with
12 applicablestateandfederallawsandregulations.
13 III. Nothing in this section shall be interpreted to disqualify any nonprofit or any not-for-
14 profit from entering into contracts for goods and services which would reasonably be provided
15 through contracts or bidding processes. Such goods and services shall be quantifiable and verifiable
16 providedastheywouldbeforanyothercontractedagency.
17 2 EffectiveDate. Thisactshalltakeeffect60daysafteritspassage.
