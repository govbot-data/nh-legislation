AMENDEDANALYSIS
Thisbill:
I. Modifiesstatutoryconstructionoftheterm"genderidentity".
II. Requires bathrooms and locker rooms in public schools and municipally owned buildings to
beusedonthebasisofsex.
III. Permits owners and operators of places of public accommodation to restrict the use of
bathroomsandlockerroomsonthebasisofsex.
IV. Requires that inmates in New Hampshire correctional facilities be housed separately on the
basisoftheirsex.
V. Modifies the enforcement of civil rights to provide protections on the basis of gender identity
onlyforcertainpurposes.
