AMENDEDANALYSIS
Thisbill:
I. Amends the standard for which New Hampshire certified appraisers can serve as supervisors
for apprentice real estate appraisers, and limits how much continuing education for applicants for
relicensing or recertification can be as a student in certain educational processes and programs
approvedbytheboard.
II. Allows for the issuance of a temporary license or certificate for individuals licensed or
certifiedinanotherstate.
