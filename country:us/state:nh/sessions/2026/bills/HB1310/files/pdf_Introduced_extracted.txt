ANALYSIS
This bill amends the standard for which New Hampshire certified appraisers can serve as
supervisors for apprentice real estate appraisers, and limits how much continuing education for
applicants for relicensing or recertification can be as a student in certain educational processes and
programsapprovedbytheboard.
Thisbillisarequestoftheofficeofprofessionallicensureandcertification.
