AMENDEDANALYSIS
Thisbill:
I. Amends the standard for which New Hampshire certified appraisers can serve as supervisors for
apprenticereal estate appraisers, and limits how much continuing education for applicants for relicensing
or recertification can be as a student in certain educational processes and programs approved by the
board.
II. Allows for the issuance of a temporary license or certificate for individuals licensed or certified in
anotherstate.
