AMENDEDANALYSIS
Thisbill:
I. Establishes the required criteria for a high school student to be awarded the New Hampshire seal
ofcivicexcellenceandengagement.
II. Authorizes school boards and school administrative unit boards to award qualifying graduating
high school students the distinction of a seal affixed to their diploma and completion of the program
referencedontheirtranscript.
III. Authorizesthestateboardofeducationtoadoptrulesrelativetotheprogram.
