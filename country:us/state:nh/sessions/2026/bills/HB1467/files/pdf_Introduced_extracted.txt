ANALYSIS
This bill requires the department of education to develop the New Hampshire Seal of Civic
Engagement and Excellence Program. The bill also authorizes school boards to award qualifying
graduating high school students the distinction of a seal affixed to their diploma and completion of
theprogramreferencedontheirtranscript.

METHODOLOGY:
This bill establishes the New Hampshire Seal of Civic Engagement and Excellence Program, to
be developed and administered by the Department of Education. The Department states it
cannot assume this responsibility with its current resources and would require one new position
(SOC23-03)toperformrequiredduties. Thepositioncostisestimated tobe$81,000inFY 2027,
$85,000 in FY 2028, and $89,000 in FY 2029. This bill provides neither an appropriation nor
authorization for new personnel. The Department adds there could be local costs incurred to
implementthisprogram,howeverthosecostsareindeterminableandwouldvarybydistrict.
