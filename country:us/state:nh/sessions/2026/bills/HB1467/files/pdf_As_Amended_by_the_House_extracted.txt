AMENDEDANALYSIS
Thisbill:
I. Establishes the required criteria for a high school student to be awarded the New Hampshire
sealofcivicexcellenceandengagement.
II. Authorizes school boards and school administrative unit boards to award qualifying
graduating high school students the distinction of a seal affixed to their diploma and completion of
theprogramreferencedontheirtranscript.
III. Authorizesthestateboardofeducationtoadoptrulesrelativetotheprogram.

2 insertingaftersection11-dthefollowingnewsection:
3 189:11-e SealofCivicExcellenceandEngagementProgram.
4 I. A school board or school administrative unit board may affix the New Hampshire state
5 seal of civic excellence and engagement on a diploma awarded to a student graduating from high
6 school, as defined in RSA 194:23. The seal shall signify a student who has achieved a high level of
7 proficiency in civics education and engagement by satisfying the established criteria. The criteria
8 shallinclude,butisnotlimitedto:
9 (a) Successful completion of civics education beyond the minimum requirements
10 prescribedbylaworadoptedlocally;
11 (b) Demonstrated proficiency in civics knowledge, including comparative forms of
12 governance, through a standardized assessment, portfolio of work, or other mastery based
13 assessmentasapprovedbytheschoolboardorschooladministrativeunitboard;and
14 (c)(1) Participation in at least one extracurricular activity or project evidencing
15 citizenship and constitutional republican government knowledge attainment as approved by the
16 schoolboardorschooladministrativeunitboard;or
17 (2) Participationinstudentgovernmentactivities.
18 II. The state board of education shall adopt rules, pursuant to RSA 541-A, relative to the
19 seal of civic excellence and engagement program in order to assist school boards and school
20 administrative unit boards in the consistent application of this section in recognizing high student
21 proficiency. Such rules shall further articulate the qualification criteria to promote rigor and
22 consistency in application statewide, including comparable expectations for civic engagement
23 activities across districts and clear definition of the civic knowledge and skills to be measured, while
24 allowingmultiplelocallydeterminedpathwaysforassessment.
