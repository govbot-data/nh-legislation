─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
AMENDEDANALYSIS
Thisbill:
I. Adoptsthestate10-yeartransportationplanfor2027-2036.
II. Provides for toll rate adjustments at specified turnpike plazas to support projects in the
2027–203610-yeartransportationplan.
III. Provides for periodic toll adjustments tied to the New Hampshire department of
transportation's projected inflation index as part of the statewide transportation improvement
program.
IV. Authorizes the transfer of certain land acquired by the state under the land conservation
investment program to the town of New Boston for the purpose of reconstructing the Gregg Mill
RoadBridge.
