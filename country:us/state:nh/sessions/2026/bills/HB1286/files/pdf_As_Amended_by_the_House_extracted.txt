1 1 New Paragraph; Practice of Dentistry; X-Rays; When Required. Amend RSA 317-A:17 by
2 insertingafterparagraphIIthefollowingnewparagraph:
3 II-a. Notwithstanding RSA 317-A:17, II(j), a dentist may treat a patient who has declined to
4 have a radiographic examination. In such cases, the dentist shall require the patient to sign a
5 waiver of liability, indicating that the patient waives the right to sue the dentist in the event that
6 the lack of a radiographic examination results in unforeseen injury to the patient. No dentist shall
7 be subject to license suspension, revocation, or any other disciplinary action by the board for actions
8 takenpursuanttothisparagraph.
