Thisbill:
I. Allows the division of motor vehicles to create a temporary traditional driver's license for
youthoperatorswhoseyouthoperatorlicensewillexpirein30daysorless.
II. Revisesregistrationrequirementsandassociatedfeesforfleetvehicles.
