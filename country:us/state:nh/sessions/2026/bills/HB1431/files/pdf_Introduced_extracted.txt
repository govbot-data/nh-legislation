ANALYSIS
This bill restricts the use of neonicotinoid pesticides and requires the department of agriculture,
markets, and food to create an educational program about the use of such pesticides and
alternatives.

13 I. The department of agriculture shall re-classify neonicotinoid pesticides as “restricted use”

16 430:61 UseofNeonicotinoidonStateProperty.
17 I. No neonicotinoid pesticides shall be used on any property owned, leased, or managed by
18 the state of New Hampshire, including but not limited to state parks, forests, recreational areas,
19 highwayrights-of-way,leasedlands,andgroundssurroundingstatebuildings,except:
20 (a) Forcontrolofinvasivespecies;
21 (b) For structural pest control within state buildings when applied by licensed pest
22 controloperators;or
23 (c) Exemptions approved bythepesticidedivisionof thedepartment of agriculture onan
24 “asneeded”basis.
25 II. Application of neonicotinoid pesticides on state property shall be limited from dusk until
26 dawn.
27 III. Neonicotinoid pesticide shall not be applied to outdoor flowering plants during bloom
