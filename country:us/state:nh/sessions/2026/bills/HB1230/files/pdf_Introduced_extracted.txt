ANALYSIS
Thisbill:
I. Requires that the state, political subdivisions of the state, or local districts cap total annual
spending, changes to tax rates, or increases in debt at a rate tied to increases in inflation and
populationchange.
II. Creates aright ofactionagainst thestate, political subdivisionsof thestate, or local districts
forpersonswhomrevealastateorlocalofficialtopreventsuchconduct.
