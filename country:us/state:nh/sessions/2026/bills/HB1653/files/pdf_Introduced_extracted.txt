ANALYSIS
This bill regulates transfer policies at freestanding hospital emergency facilities (FHEFs). The
bill provides that patients receiving emergency medical care at FHEFs shall have the option to
transfer to the acute care hospital of their choice and shall not be limited solely to the facility that
ownsoroperatestheFHEF.
