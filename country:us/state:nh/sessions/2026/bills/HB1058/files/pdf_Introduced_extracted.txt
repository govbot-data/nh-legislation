2 215-A:20 LoadedFirearmsForbidden.
3 No person shall carry on an OHRV, or a trailer towed by same, any firearms unless said firearm
4 is unloaded. This section shall not apply to law enforcement officers carrying firearms in the course
5 of duty, or to pistols or revolvers carried by any person who is not prohibited from possessing a
6 firearm by a New Hampshire statute. This section shall not apply when the OHRV is being
7 operated on private property where the operator has the express or implied permission or
8 license of the landowner or lawful occupant to be present, provided that such permission
9 does not arise solely by way of a public right-of-way, public trail, or designated OHRV
10 trail/corridor. For the purposes of this section, “unloaded” shall mean that the firearm is
11 not loaded as defined in RSA 207:1, XIV-a. A firearm shall not be considered to be loaded
12 merely because a magazine, tube, or other ammunition storage device attached to the
13 firearmcontainsammunition,solongasnoroundischambered.
