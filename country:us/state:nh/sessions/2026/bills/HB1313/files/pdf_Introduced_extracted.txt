BeitEnactedbytheSenateandHouseofRepresentativesinGeneralCourtconvened:
1 1 ShortTitle. Thisactshallbeknownas"TheSidewalkFreeSpeechAct."
2 2 FindingsandPurpose. Thegeneralcourtfindsthat:
3 I. Theexerciseofaperson’srighttofreespeechisafirstamendmentactivity,andonewhich
4 the New Hampshire Bill of Rights is especially zealous in protecting. N.H. const. part 1 art. 22
5 (stating that “Free speech… ought… to be inviolably preserved.”) The right to free speech protects
6 viewpointswhicharecontroversial,unpopular,andoffensiveorotherwiseinconvenient.
7 II. RSA132:37throughRSA132:40wouldinfringeuponfreespeech.
8 III. The United States Supreme Court has observed that “‘one-on-one communication’ is ‘the
9 most effective, fundamental, and perhaps economical avenue of political discourse.’… ‘handing out
10 leaflets in the advocacy of a politically controversial viewpoint... is the essence of First Amendment
11 expression’; ‘[n]o form of speech is entitled to greater constitutional protection.’… When the
12 government makes it more difficult to engage in these modes of communication, it imposes an
13 especially significant First Amendment burden.” McCullen v. Coakley, 573 U.S. 464, 488-489 (2014)
14 (citing Meyer v. Grant, 486 U.S. 414, 424, (1988); McIntyre v. Ohio Elections Comm’n, 514 U.S. 334,
15 347(1995)).
16 IV. RSA 132:37 through RSA 132:40, if ever implemented through the demarcation of a
17 buffer zone, would be subject to immediate constitutional challenge. Reddy v. Foster, (stating that
18 plaintiffs will have standing to challenge the constitutionality of the law once an abortion clinic
19 “demarcate[s]azone.”)
20 V. If subject to constitutional challenge, RSA 132:37 through RSA 132:40 is likely to be, and
21 ought to be, struck down under either the federal or state Bills of Rights. McCullen v. Coakley, 573
22 U.S.464(2014)(correctlyinvalidatingMassachusettsbufferzonesundertheFirstAmendment).
23 VI. RSA132:37throughRSA132:40hasservednopublicpurpose.
24 3 Repeal. RSA 132:37 through 132:40, relative to access to reproductive health care facilities,
25 arerepealed.
26 4 EffectiveDate. Thisactshalltakeeffectuponitspassage.
