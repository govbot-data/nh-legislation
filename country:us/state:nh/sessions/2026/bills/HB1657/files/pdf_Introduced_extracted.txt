ANALYSIS
This bill directs state agencies to identify vacant positions and provide such information to the
joint committee on employee classification, and following review by the joint committee, legislation
toabolishthepositionmaybeproposedbythejointcommittee.

1 1 New Paragraph; Joint Committee on Employee Classification; Policy Regarding Vacant
2 Positions. AmendRSA14:14-cbyinsertingafterparagraphIVthefollowingnewparagraph:
3 V. The acting administrative head of each state agency shall identify, on a rolling basis, all
4 full and part-time classified and unclassified positions in the agency that have been vacant for at
5 least 90 days and report such information to the joint committee on employee classification, the
6 governor, the speaker of the house of representatives, the president of the senate, the fiscal
7 committee of the general court, the chairmen of the house and senate executive departments and
8 administration committees, and the executive council. The report shall include a spreadsheet
9 including the agency; classified or unclassified status; pay grade; position code; funded or unfunded
10 status; allocation of funding for the position between state general funds, federal funds, or specific
11 state funds; date the position became vacant; days vacant as of the report and the accounting unit
12 description. Any full or part-time classified or unclassified position in an agency that has been
13 vacant for at least 90 days shall not be filled without express approval of the governor and the
14 executivecouncil.
15 2 EffectiveDate. Thisactshalltakeeffect60daysafteritspassage.
