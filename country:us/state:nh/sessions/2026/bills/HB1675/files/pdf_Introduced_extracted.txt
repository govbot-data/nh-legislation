ANALYSIS
This bill establishes a commission to investigate the New Hampshire Coalition Against Sexual
and Domestic Violence, subjects thecoalition to annual audits, requiringfunding onlybe provided to
the coalition for services materially benefitting survivors of sexual and domestic assault, and
removesthecoalition'smembershiponanystatecommissions.
