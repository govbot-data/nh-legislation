ANACT directing the department of health and human services to seek clarification from
the Administration for Children and Families regarding the use of TANF reserve
funds and repealing the requirement that the department of health and human
services' biennial budget request include funding for certain child care workforce
programs.
SPONSORS: Rep. Wallner, Merr. 19; Rep. Muns, Rock. 29; Rep. D. Paige, Carr. 1; Rep. M.
Smith, Straf. 10; Rep. Simpson, Rock. 33; Rep. Telerski, Hills. 11; Rep. Preece,

COMMITTEE: Finance
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
AMENDEDANALYSIS
The bill directs the department to seek clarification from the federal government regarding the
use of TANF reserve funds and to request a waiver for certain uses of such funds that the federal
government determines as prohibited uses. This bill also repeals the requirement that the
departmentincludefundingforcertainchildcareworkforceprogramsinitsbiennialbudgetrequest.

ANACT directing the department of health and human services to seek clarification from
the Administration for Children and Families regarding the use of TANF reserve
funds and repealing the requirement that the department of health and human
services' biennial budget request include funding for certain child care workforce
programs.
BeitEnactedbytheSenateandHouseofRepresentativesinGeneralCourtconvened:
1 1 Request for Clarification Regarding Use of TANF Reserve Funds. No later than August 1,
2 2026, the department of health and human services shall seek clarification from the federal
3 Administration for Children and Families on the specific uses to which federal TANF reserve funds
4 may be put, as well as any conditions surrounding their transfer to other funds, including the Child
5 Care and Development Fund. The department shall specifically seek clarification regarding the
6 ways in which TANF reserve funds may be used to support recruitment and retention of child care
7 employees, including any alternatives to employment bonuses paid to providers. Within one month
8 of receipt of the Administration's response, the department shall submit said response to the joint
9 fiscal committee and the house and senate finance committees. If the Administration determines
10 that such funds shall not be used for the purposes of this act, the department shall seek a waiver
11 from any requirements prohibiting such use of the funds. The department shall seek such waiver
12 within one month of the Administration's determination that TANF reserve funds shall not be used
13 forthepurposesofthisact.
14 2 Repeal. 2025, 141:431, VI, directing the department of health and human services to include
15 the amount necessary to fully fund certain child care workforce programs in its biennial budget
16 request,isrepealed.
17 3 EffectiveDate. ThisactshalltakeeffectJuly1,2026.
