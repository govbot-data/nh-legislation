BeitEnactedbytheSenateandHouseofRepresentativesinGeneralCourtconvened:
1 1 StatementofFindings. Thegeneralcourtherebyfindsthat:
2 I. New Hampshire’s forests and urban trees provide critical benefits, including filtering air
3 andwater,interceptingrainfall,andreducingerosion.
4 II. Local tree canopy contributes to stormwater management and protects the quality of
5 rivers,lakes,andaquifersrelieduponforpublicdrinkingwater.
6 III. Tree canopy also contributes to property values, neighborhood character, and the health
7 and comfort of residents, including seniors and children, by providing shade and reducing excess
8 heat.
9 IV. Municipalities across the state have raised concerns about tree canopy loss and
10 requestedclearauthoritytoincludecanopypreservationprovisionsinzoningandmasterplanning.
11 2 New Section; Local Land Use Planning; Zoning; Tree Canopy Preservation Authorized.
12 AmendRSA674byinsertingaftersection16-athefollowingnewsection:
13 674:16-b TreeCanopyPreservationAuthorized.
14 I. A municipality may, at its discretion, adopt zoning ordinances or master plan provisions
15 providing for the preservation, management, or replacement of tree canopy. Such zoning ordinances
16 ormasterplanprovisionsmayaddressthefollowing:
17 (a) Retentionofexistingtreesduringdevelopment;
18 (b) Replacementorplantingrequirementswherecanopyisremoved;and
19 (c) Long-termcanopymanagementgoalsaspartofthemunicipalmasterplan.
20 II. Nothing in this section shall be construed to require any municipality to adopt such
21 ordinancesorprovisions.
22 3 EffectiveDate. Thisactshalltakeeffect60daysafteritspassage.
