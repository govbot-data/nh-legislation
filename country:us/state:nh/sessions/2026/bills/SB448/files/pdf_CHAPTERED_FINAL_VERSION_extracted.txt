AMENDEDANALYSIS
This bill changes the definition of veteran and expands eligibility for state benefits and programs that
depend on veteran status, ensuring that individuals discharged under honorable conditions, including
thosewithageneraldischargeunderhonorableconditions,arenotexcludedfromeligibility.
