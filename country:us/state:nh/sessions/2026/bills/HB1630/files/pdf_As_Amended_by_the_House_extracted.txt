AMENDEDANALYSIS
This bill prohibits licensees from using their premises for illegal purposes, requires compliance
with laws on inhaling toxic vapors, and bans selling nitrous oxide devices, with violations subject to
enforcementbytheliquorcommissionandrepeatoffensesleadingtolicenserevocation.
