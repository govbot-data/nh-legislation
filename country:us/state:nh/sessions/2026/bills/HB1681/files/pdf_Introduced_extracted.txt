ANALYSIS
Thisbillestablishesstandardsforinnovativehousingstructuressuchastinyhouses,tinyhouses
on wheels, and yurts, allowing their use as single-family or accessory dwellings, setting inspection
andpermittingrequirements,authorizingmunicipalpropertytaxassessments,regulatingtransport,
andpermittinggreywaterdisposalsystems.
