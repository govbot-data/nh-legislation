ANALYSIS
This bill requires the preservation of written public comments submitted on legislation,
including those received via hearings and the state’s remote sign-in system, and mandates that
standing committees prepare reports explaining how such comments influenced their
recommendations. These reports must be submitted to the clerk and retained in the permanent
recordofthebill.
