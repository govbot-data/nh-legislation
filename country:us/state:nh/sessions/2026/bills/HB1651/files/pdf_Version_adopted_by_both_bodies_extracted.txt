METHODOLOGY:
This bill creates a new type of protective order petition in Circuit Court for victims of sexual
assault under RSA 632-A. The Judicial Branch states they would need to modify court forms,
their website and systems as well as provide limited training to staff for the new petition type.
ThefiscalimpacttotheBranchwouldbeunder$10,000.
Additionally, this bill adds, deletes, or modifies a criminal penalty, or changes statute to which
there is a penalty for violation. Therefore, this bill may have an impact on the judicial and
correctional systems, which could affect prosecution, incarceration, probation, and parole costs,
forthestate,aswellascountyandlocalgovernments. Asummaryofsuchcostscanbefound at:
https://gencourt.state.nh.us/lba/Budget/Fiscal_Notes/JudicialCorrectionalCosts.pdf
