ANALYSIS
Thisbill:
I. Defines"politicalaffiliationoropinion."
II. Prohibitsdiscriminationorharassmentofindividualsbasedonpoliticalaffiliationoropinion.
III. Prohibitspoliticallymotivatedinterferencewithaperson'seconomicinterests.
IV. Providespenaltiesforviolations.
