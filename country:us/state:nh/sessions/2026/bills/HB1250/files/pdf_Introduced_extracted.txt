SPONSORS: Rep.Berry,Hills.44
COMMITTEE: Labor,IndustrialandRehabilitativeServices
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ANALYSIS
This bill clarifies that employees shall give at least 15 days’ notice to take leave for childbirth-
related medical appointments and allows employers to deny job reinstatement if doing so would
undulydisruptoperations.

3 275:37-f Leave of Absence to Attend Medical Appointments for Childbirth, Postpartum Care,
4 and Infant Pediatric Medical Appointments. No employer with 20 employees or more, shall deny an
5 employee leave from work up to a total of 25 hours to attend the employee's own medical
6 appointments for childbirth, postpartum care, or the employee's child's pediatric medical
7 appointments within the first year of the child's birth or adoption. In the case where both parents of
8 a child are employees of the same employer, the parents collectively may take unpaid leave
9 according to this section, for a total of 25 hours in their child's first year. An employer is not
10 required to pay an employee for any time taken as leave pursuant to this section. However, an
11 employee shall be permitted to substitute any accrued vacation time or other appropriate paid leave
12 for any leave taken pursuant to this section provided doing so does not violate any terms of
13 employment. When the employee returns from their own or their child's health appointments, that
14 employee's original job shall be made available to the employee by the employer if the job is still
15 available and doing so would not unduly disrupt the operations of the employer. An
16 employee who wishes to request leave under this section shall provide [reasonable] at least 15
17 calendar days' notice to the employer prior to the leave and make a reasonable effort to schedule
18 the leave so as not to unduly disrupt the operations of the employer. An employer may ask for, and
19 the employee shall then provide, documentation from the employee to ensure the time is being
20 usedforitsintendedpurpose.
21 2 EffectiveDate. ThisactshalltakeeffectJanuary1,2027.
