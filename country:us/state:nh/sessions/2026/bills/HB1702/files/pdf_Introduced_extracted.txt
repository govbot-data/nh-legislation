ANALYSIS
Thisbill:
I. Defines"chemical"and"medicalabortion"drugs.
II. Requires medical facilities and pharmacies that provides chemical abortion drugs to post a
standardizednoticeregardingthepotentialreversibilityofmifepristone.
III. Establishes signage requirements by facility type, imposes a class B felony for intentional
noncompliance,andauthorizescivilactionsforfailuretoprovidetherequirednotice.
