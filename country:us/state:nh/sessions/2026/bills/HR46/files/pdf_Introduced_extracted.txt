─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ANALYSIS
This resolution establishes a state day of remembrance on the first Friday of June to honor
children from New Hampshire who have lost their lives to gun violence, directs ceremonial
observances, calls for a permanent memorial in the state house, and urges Congress to create a
nationaldayofremembrance.
