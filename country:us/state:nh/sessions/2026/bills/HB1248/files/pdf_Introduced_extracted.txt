ANALYSIS
This bill adds offenses based on the operation of a motor vehicle for which the defendant was
convicted, and as condition of that conviction, was sentenced to an alcohol or controlled substance,
education, treatment, or counseling program, within 10 years preceding the date of the second or
subsequentoffense,asarequisitepriorconvictionforenhancedDWIpenalties.
