BeitEnactedbytheSenateandHouseofRepresentativesinGeneralCourtconvened:
1 1 StatementofFindingsandPurpose. Thegeneralcourtherebyfindsthat:
2 I. The New Hampshire Municipal Association (NHMA) is a private nonprofit corporation
3 whose membership is composed primarily of New Hampshire municipalities and which is funded
4 substantiallythroughmunicipalduesderivedfromtaxpayerfunds.
5 II. NHMA engages in legislative advocacy, lobbying, legal support, training, and other
6 activitiesthatmateriallyinfluencepublicpolicyinthisstate.
7 III. Despite its public funding base and influence, NHMA is not currently subject to any
8 oversight,transparency,orreportingobligationstothestateortothegeneralcourt.
9 IV. Taxpayers have a legitimate interest in ensuring that municipal dues are spent
10 transparently and that lobbying activities are fully disclosed and not subsidized with public funds
11 withoutaccountability.
12 V. Therefore, the purpose of this act is to establish a legislative commission on municipal
13 association oversight to ensure transparency, accountability, and legislative oversight of the
14 activitiesofNHMA.
15 2 New Chapter; Legislative Commission on Municipal Association Oversight. Amend RSA by
16 insertingafterchapter17-Sthefollowingnewchapter:

19 17-T:1 CommissionEstablished.
20 I. Thereisestablishedalegislativecommissiontobeknownasthelegislativecommissionon
21 municipal association oversight. The purpose of the commission is to provide legislative oversight of
22 the finances, activities, and use of public funds by the New Hampshire Municipal Association
23 (NHMA).
24 II. Thecommissionshallconsistof7votingmembersasfollows:
25 (a) Three members of the New Hampshire house of representatives, appointed by the
26 speakerofthehouseofrepresentatives,including:
27 (1) OnememberoftheNewHampshirehousefinancecommittee;
28 (2) One member of the New Hampshire house municipal and county government
29 committee;and
30 (3) Oneothermemberofthehouseofrepresentatives.
31 (b) TwomembersoftheNewHampshiresenate,appointedbythesenatepresident.
