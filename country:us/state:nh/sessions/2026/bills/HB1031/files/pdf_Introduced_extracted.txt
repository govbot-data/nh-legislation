ANACT enabling candidates for state office to use campaign funds to pay for security

BeitEnactedbytheSenateandHouseofRepresentativesinGeneralCourtconvened:
1 1 New Section; Candidate and State Official Security. Amend RSA 664 by inserting after
2 section11thefollowingnewsection:
3 664:11-a Candidate and State Official Security. The use of up to $3,000 of campaign funds to
4 pay for the reasonable costs of security measures for a state candidate, state officeholder, member of
5 theirfamily, and employeesof thecandidate'scampaignor thestateofficeholder's office,shall not be
6 considered personal use, so long as the security measures address ongoing dangers or threats that
7 would not exist irrespective of the individual's status or duties as a state candidate or state
8 officeholder. Disbursements for security measures shall befor theusual and normal charge for such
9 goodsorservices. Inthissection,"usualandnormalcharge"means,inthecaseofgoods,thepriceof
10 those goods in the market in which they are ordinarily purchased, and, in the case of services, the
11 hourly or piecework charge for the services at a commercially reasonable rate prevailing at the time
12 theserviceswererendered. Suchsecuritymeasuresinclude,butarenotlimitedto:
13 I. Non-structural security devices, such as security hardware, locks, alarm systems, motion
14 detectors,andsecuritycamerasystems;
15 II. Structural security devices, such as wiring, lighting, gates, doors, and fencing, so long as
16 such devices are intended solely to provide security and not to improve the property or increase its
17 value;
18 III. Securitypersonnelandservicesthatarebonafide,legitimate,andprofessional;and
19 IV. Cybersecuritysoftware,devices,andservices.
20 2 EffectiveDate. Thisactshalltakeeffect60daysafteritspassage.
