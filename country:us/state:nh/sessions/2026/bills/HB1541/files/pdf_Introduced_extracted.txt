METHODOLOGY:
This bill requires the Secretary of State to provide all voting precincts with secure, durable,
reusable, and tamper-evident containers for storing ballots. These containers must be lockable,
stackable, transportable, and individually numbered. Help America Vote Act (HAVA) Funds
shallbeusedforimplementationofthesecurecontainerreplacementprogram. Additionally,the
Secretary of State shall provide an annual progress report to include the number of secure
containersreplaced,locations,andplannedcontainerreplacementforthefollowingyear.
The Department of State indicates the bill would necessitate the purchase and distribution of
these containers and related materials before each election. The containers would need to
accommodate various ballot sizes and quantities, requiring custom manufacturing. The
Secretary of State anticipates needing enough containers for five elections initially, after which
only replacements or additional units would be necessary. The fiscal impact is indeterminable
due to unknown costs for manufacturing, shipping, and potential funding limitations. There are
no funds currentlyappropriated for this initiative, and theSecretary of Statewould need to seek
clarification from the Election Assistance Commission (EAC) on whether Help America Vote Act
(HAVA)fundscanbeusedforthesepurchases.
