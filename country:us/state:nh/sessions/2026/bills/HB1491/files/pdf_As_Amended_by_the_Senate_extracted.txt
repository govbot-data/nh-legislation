AMENDEDANALYSIS
This bill establishes and regulates political subdivision risk management programs, sets eligibility
and reporting requirements, defines oversight and enforcement authority, outlines financial,
investment, and solvency standards, and affirms tax exemptions and confidentiality provisions for
suchprograms.
