ANALYSIS
This bill modifies theright-to-know law byrequiringthat theonlyminutes or decisions withheld
from public disclosure are those portions covered by the motion to enter nonpublic session. The bill
also adds start times and end times in the list of nonpublic session minutes available under the
right-to-knowlaw.
