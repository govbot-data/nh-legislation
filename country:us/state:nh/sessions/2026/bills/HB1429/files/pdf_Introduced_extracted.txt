METHODOLOGY:
This bill allows polling places where the moderator believes that the ballot counting will extend
beyond 12:00 a.m. to pause the ballot counting no earlier than 11:00 p.m. The bill outlines
procedures for securely storing ballots overnight, including the presence of election officials or
law enforcement, completion of a chain of custody form, and allows up to five members of the
public to be present in the room where the ballots and materials are held for the overnight time
period. It also requires video and audio recording of the storage area and if possible, a live
broadcastofthescene.
The Department of State indicates that this bill conflicts with various existing statutes
(specifically RSA 659:63 & RSAs 659:77-659:79) that currently require vote counting and
tabulation to be completed without adjournment and mandate timely submission of election
returns. Delaying ballot counting could conflict with these laws, potentially triggering legal
consequences. The Secretary of State anticipates the need for extensive legal consultation with
the Attorney General’s office and additional training for election officials. Due to these
complexities,thefiscalimpactisanindeterminableincreasetostateexpenditures.
ItisassumedthatanyfiscalimpactwouldoccurafterFY2026.
