BeitEnactedbytheSenateandHouseofRepresentativesinGeneralCourtconvened:
1 1 New Section; Lease Provisions; Snow and Ice Removal. Amend RSA 540 by inserting after
2 section28thefollowingnewsection:
3 540:28-a SnowandIceRemovalinTenancies.
4 I. The landlord and tenant may agree that either the landlord or the tenant, or any
5 combination thereof, shall be responsible for performing snow and ice removal from, and
6 maintainingsafeaccessto,specificareasoftherentalproperty.
7 II. Unless otherwise agreed, the responsibility shall rest with the landlord of restricted
8 property for clearing snow and ice from, and maintaining safe access to, on-site common areas used
9 by tenants of more than one rental unit, including but not limited to, driveways, walkways, stairs
10 and entryways. Unless otherwise agreed, the responsibility for clearing snow and ice from, and
11 maintaining safe access to, private areas, including but not limited to, walkways, stairs, and
12 entryways intended for exclusive use of one rental unit, shall rest with the tenants who have such
13 exclusiveuse.
14 III. Nothing in this section shall be construed to alter snow and ice removal responsibilities
15 for areas owned, maintained, or governed by a condominium association, cooperative, homeowners’
16 association, manufactured housing park, or similar entity in which there is a mixture of private and
17 common ownership, and pursuant to recorded instruments, bylaws, park rules, or other governing
18 documents.
19 2 Applicability. This act shall apply to all tenancies entered into or renewed on or after the
20 effectivedateofthisact.
21 3 EffectiveDate. ThisactshalltakeeffectJuly1,2027.
