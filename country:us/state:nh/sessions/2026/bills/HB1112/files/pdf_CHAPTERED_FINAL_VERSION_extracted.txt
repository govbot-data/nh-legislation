1 213:1 New Section; Lease Provisions; Snow and Ice Removal. Amend RSA 540 by inserting after
2 section28thefollowingnewsection:
3 540:28-a SnowandIceRemovalinTenancies.
4 I. The landlord and tenant may agree that either the landlord or the tenant, or any combination
5 thereof, shall be responsible for performing snow and ice removal from, and maintaining safe access to,
6 specificareasoftherentalproperty.
7 II. Unlessotherwiseagreed,theresponsibilityshallrestwiththelandlordofrestrictedpropertyfor
8 clearing snow and ice from, and maintaining safe access to, on-site common areas used by tenants of
9 morethanonerentalunit,includingbutnotlimitedto,driveways,walkways,stairsandentryways. Unless
10 otherwise agreed, the responsibility for clearing snow and ice from, and maintaining safe access to,
11 private areas, including but not limited to, walkways, stairs, and entryways intended for exclusive use of
12 onerentalunit,shallrestwiththetenantswhohavesuchexclusiveuse.
13 III. Nothing in this section shall be construed to alter snow and ice removal responsibilities for
14 areas owned, maintained, or governed by a condominium association, cooperative, homeowners’
15 association, manufactured housing park, or similar entity in which there is a mixture of private and
16 common ownership, and pursuant to recorded instruments, bylaws, park rules, or other governing
17 documents.
18 213:2 Applicability. This act shall apply to all tenancies entered into or renewed on or after the
19 effectivedateofthisact.
213:3 EffectiveDate. ThisactshalltakeeffectJuly1,2027.
