BeitEnactedbytheSenateandHouseofRepresentativesinGeneralCourtconvened:
1 1 New Section; Landlord and Tenant; Snow and Ice Removal. Amend RSA 540-A by inserting
2 aftersection3-athefollowingnewsection:
3 540-A:3-b SnowandIceRemovalinResidentialTenancies.
4 I. In a residential tenancy, responsibility for snow and ice removal shall be explicitly stated
5 inthewrittenlease.
6 II. Unless otherwise assigned in the lease, responsibility for maintaining safe access to and
7 clearing snow and ice from common areas, including driveways, walkways, and entryways used by
8 multipletenants,shallrestwiththepropertyownerorlandlord.
9 III. The landlord and tenant may agree in writing that the tenant shall perform snow and
10 ice removal for specific areas, provided the agreement is express, voluntary, and included in the
11 lease.
12 IV. Nothing in this section shall affect any local ordinance imposing additional duties on
13 propertyownersoroccupantsregardingpublicsidewalksorrightsofway.
14 2 Applicability. This act shall apply to all residential leases entered into or renewed on or after
15 theeffectivedateofthissection.
16 3 EffectiveDate. ThisactshalltakeeffectJanuary1,2027.
