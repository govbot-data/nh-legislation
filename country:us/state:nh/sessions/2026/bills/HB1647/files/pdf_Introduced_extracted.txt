ANALYSIS
This bill requires all municipalities, cities, and towns to assess qualifying farm structures for no
more than their replacement costs, less depreciation, and the land under the qualifying farm
structuresat no morethan10percent of itsmarket value and sets procedures and penalties for non-
payment.
