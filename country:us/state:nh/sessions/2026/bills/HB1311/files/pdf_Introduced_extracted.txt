ANALYSIS
This bill establishes a regulatory analysis fund to support economic impact reviews of proposed
administrative rules and authorizes the joint legislative committee to hire external analysts and
makesanappropriationtherefor.

4 I. There is established in the office of the state treasurer a nonlapsing fund to be known as

7 general court indetermining theeconomic impact of rules proposed under this chapter on the state's

10 the office of legislative services, to hire an external economic analyst to evaluate the impact of rules
11 proposedunderthischapter,subjecttotheavailabilityoffundswithintheregulatoryanalysisfund.

METHODOLOGY:
This bill establishes a nonlapsing fund called the Regulatory Analysis Fund to allow the Joint
Legislative Committee on Administrative Rules to contract with external economic analysts for
evaluating the economic impact of proposed administrative rules. It includes an appropriation of
$1forFY2026toinitiatethefund.
ItisassumedthatanyfiscalimpactwouldoccurafterFY2026.
