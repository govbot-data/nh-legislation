ANALYSIS
This resolution urges the United States Congress to rescind President Donald Trump's tariffs by
terminating the national state of emergency declared by President Trump on April 2, 2025. The
resolution also urges the United States Congress to oppose all future declarations of states of
emergency through the International Emergency Economic Powers Act that would permit future
tariffincreasesbyPresidentTrump.
