ANALYSIS
This bill requires school boards to develop and adopt a policy to prevent the questioning of minor
students by non-school employees and certain school employees without prior written permission of the
parents or guardians, and such policy shall require that an adult student be asked if they want their
parentsorguardiansnotifiedbeforeanysuchquestioning.
