METHODOLOGY:
This bill creates a process allowing a petitioner to request that the Circuit Court, District
Division issue an extreme risk protection order requiring a respondent who poses an immediate
danger to self or others to surrender firearms. The bill outlines procedures for search warrants,
multiple hearings on short timelines, review hearings, and data reporting requirements, and
mandatestheuseofspecializedformsandtranslatedmaterials.
The Judicial Branch states the bill will create a new and complex case type for the District
Division that is expected to generate an indeterminable but likely significant number of new
filings each year. Because the bill requires rapid scheduling of hearings, short decision
timelines, and multiple review hearings, the Branch anticipates substantial additional workload
impacts. These accelerated timelines will displace existing scheduled matters and contribute to
schedulingdelaysacrossother casetypes. Thebillalsorequires developmentanddistributionof
specialized forms, the creation of handbooks, guidelines, and informational brochures,
translation of required documents, new data entry requirements into a statewide database, and
preparation of an annual report. The Branch notes that these responsibilities will require
ongoing training for judges and staff. To manage these obligations, the Judicial Branch
anticipates the need for at least one additional full-time equivalent employee to administer the

program, coordinate training, and support increased case flow. Because the number of petitions
that will be filed is unknown, the total cost is indeterminable, but the Branch estimates that
Generalfundexpenditureswillbebetween$500,000to$1,000,000. Theeffectivedateofthis bill
isJanuary1,2027sotheassumptionistheGeneralFundexpenditureimpactforFY2027would
be half the cost at $250,000 to $500,000. This bill does not provide funding or authorization of
newpositions.
