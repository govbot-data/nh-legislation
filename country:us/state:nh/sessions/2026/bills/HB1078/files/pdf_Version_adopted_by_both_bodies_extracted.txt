METHODOLOGY:
This bill creates gold star father and gold star family motor vehicle license plates. The
Department of Safety states this would require system changes, at a cost of approximately
$25,000 and the creation of a new plate would range in cost from $3,000 to $30,000, depending
on the number of plates that need to be manufactured. In total, this bill is expected to increase
stateexpendituresby$28,000to$55,000inFY2027.
