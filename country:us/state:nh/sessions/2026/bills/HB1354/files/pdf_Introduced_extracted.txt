METHODOLOGY:
This bill, effective January 1, 2027, creates a new plate for passenger and motorcycle style
vehicles, bearing a symbol representing amateur radio operators. The plate registration fee
wouldbe$85,whichafterdeductingproductionandadministrativecosts,shallbecreditedtothe
state highway fund. The Department of Safety estimates a one-time system change cost of
approximately $25,000 and a one-time cost, for the creation of the new plate type, of $3,000 to
$30,000, for a total cost of $28,000 to $55,000. Based on this estimate, it would take
approximately 330 to 650 registrations of the new plate type, at $85, to recover implementation
costs. Since the number of registrations cannot be predicted, this bill’s impact on highway fund
revenueisindeterminable.
