AMENDEDANALYSIS
Thisbill:
I. Directs the department of health and human services to include in certain materials relative to
childhood immunization requirements a statement indicating that medical and religious exemptions to
suchrequirementsareavailableunderstatelaw.
II. Requires pharmacies to make efforts to notify consumers of their right to request the lowest
availablepriceforprescriptiondrugs.
