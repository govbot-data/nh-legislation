METHODOLOGY:
This bill requires the Department of Health and Human Services to provide notice of medical
and religious exemptions from immunization requirements. The Department assumes that in
the first year of implementation, it will need a part-time medical health services specialist to
communicate the changes to the several hundred impacted providers. The cost for this position
is estimated to be $43,500. The Department assumes it will take up to nine months to fully
comply with the bill's requirements, including the time needed to develop and implement
administrativerules.
