2 490-D:3 Equity Jurisdiction. [Notwithstanding any law to the contrary, the] The judicial
3 branch family division shall have the powers of a court of equity in cases where subject matter
4 jurisdiction lies with the judicial branch family division and where no other special provision
5 has been made by law. Suits in equity where subject matter jurisdiction lies with the judicial
6 branch family division including, but not limited to, petitions for divorce, nullity of marriage,
7 alimony, custody of children, support, and other similar proceedings may be heard upon oral
8 testimony or depositions, or both, or when both parties consent, or service having been made and a
9 notice of the time and place of the hearing having been given, when both parties appear. Such suits
10 maybeheardbyanyjusticeof thejudicialbranchfamilydivisionat anytime,but nothingcontained
11 in this section shall be construed as limiting the power of the judicial branch family division to have
12 issues of fact framed and tried by a jury, according to the rules in equity, or the course of such
13 proceedingsatcommonlaw.
14 2 EffectiveDate. ThisactshalltakeeffectJanuary1,2027.
