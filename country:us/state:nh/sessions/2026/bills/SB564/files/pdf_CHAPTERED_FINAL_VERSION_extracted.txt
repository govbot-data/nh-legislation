AMENDEDANALYSIS
Thisbill:
I. Prohibits municipalities from placing limits on maximum road length, provided that the proposed
roadwayorextensionfullycomplieswithstatefirecode.
II. Prohibits municipalitiesfrom imposing acap on thenumber of housinglots on adead-end road or
street, unless such cap is in compliance with state fire code and National Fire Protection Association
standard 1141, and provided that the proposed lots otherwise comply with current zoning restrictions and
requirements.
III. Requires municipalities to allow the placement of utilities within or along buffer areas, or wetland
buffersorconservationareasofasubdivisionorlotbyaspecialexceptionorconditionalusepermit.
IV. Prohibitsmunicipalitiesfromimposingadditionalrestrictionsorrequirementsregardingbuildingor
lotsizeforaffectedpropertiesbeforetheeffectivedateoftheact.
