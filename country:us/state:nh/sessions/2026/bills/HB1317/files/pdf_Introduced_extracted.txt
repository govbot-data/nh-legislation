ANALYSIS
This bill provides that no state agency, political subdivision, contractor, vendor, or grant
recipient shall collect, maintain, or disclose personally identifiable medical, disability, or mental
health data to the federal government or any third party, except in limited circumstances as
permittedunderstateandfederallaw.
