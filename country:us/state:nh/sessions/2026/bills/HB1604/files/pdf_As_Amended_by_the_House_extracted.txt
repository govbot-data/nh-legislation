METHODOLOGY:
This bill requires any suitable town, city, or school owned/operated facility within the town or
ward, such as public schools, town halls, fire stations, or other municipal or, school district, or
charter school property, be made available as polling places upon request by the selectmen of a
town or the ward officers of a city ward. The municipality would be responsible for reasonable
costs associated with custodial services, utilities, and security unless otherwise agreed upon by
therelevantpartiesinvolved.
The New Hampshire Municipal Association states adding an additional polling location could
increase local expenditures by approximately $10,000 to $100,000, primarily for staffing and
essential services. Furthermore, legal expenses may arise if disputes occur between facility and
municipalofficialsregardingwhatconstitutesreasonablecosts.
ItisassumedthatanyfiscalimpactwouldoccurafterFY2026.
