ANALYSIS
This bill exempts child rearing consistent with the child's biological sex from being considered
within the definition of abused child and child endangerment. The bill also removes child rearing
consistent with the child's biological sex from being a basis for findings relative to family law
determinations, including: adoptions, child-placing agency licensing, and best interests of the child
determinations.
