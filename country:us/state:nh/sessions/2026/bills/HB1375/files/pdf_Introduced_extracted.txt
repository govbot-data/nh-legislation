HOUSEBILL
ANACT prohibiting landlords from charging more than one application fee per tenant
every12months.
SPONSORS: Rep.Read,Rock.10;Rep.Wheeler,Hills.33
COMMITTEE: Housing
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ANALYSIS
This bill prohibits landlords from collecting more than one application fee from the same
prospective tenant within a 12-month period, regardless of how many units the tenant applies for or
leases.
