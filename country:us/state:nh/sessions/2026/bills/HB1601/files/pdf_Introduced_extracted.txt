METHODOLOGY:
This bill directs the Secretary of State to establish a voter education program to inform the
public about recent changes to voting laws. To support this initiative, the Secretary shall
allocate$50,000fromHelpAmericaVoteAct(HAVA)funds.
The Department of State notes that the Secretary already operates a comprehensive voter
education program. It further indicates that restricting the initiative to $50,000 may limit the
program’soutreach.
