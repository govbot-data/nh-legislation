METHODOLOGY:
This bill establishes the position of academic research and improvement performance data
analyst in the Department of Education. The Department anticipates the position (SOC 15-05,
starting step 5) to cost $101,000 in FY 2027, $103,000 in FY 2028, and $108,000 in FY 2029.
This bill provides general funds of $160,000 in FY 2027 to fund the position. Any unexpended
amounts would lapse at June 30, 2027, and it is assumed this position, were it to be established,
would have its funding considered in FY 2028 and beyond as part of the state operating budget
process.
