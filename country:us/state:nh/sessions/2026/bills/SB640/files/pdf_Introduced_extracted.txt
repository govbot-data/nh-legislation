ANALYSIS
Thisbill:
I. Prohibits the use of artificial intelligence or other means to provide services requiring a
professionallicenseunlessthoseservicesareprovidedbysomeonewithsuchalicense.
II. Allows the office of professional licensure and certification to collect fees for the costs of
enforcementoftheprohibitiononunlicensedpractice.
III. Establishes specific requirements for the use of artificial intelligence for psychologists,
mentalhealthpractitioners,andalcoholandotherdruguseprofessionals.

3 310:13 UnlicensedPractice.
4 I. Whoever, including but not limited to individuals and entities, not being licensed or

6 a profession licensed by the office of professional licensure and certification, engages in activity

11 licensure, request to see the license of anyone who advertises oneself as engaging in a profession
12 licensed by the office, engages in activity requiring professional licensure, or in any way holds

15 place of business, or where such business is being conducted, of such individual. After providing
16 notice and opportunity to be heard in accordance with RSA 310:10, and upon making an affirmative
17 finding of unlawful practice, the board may impose a fine not to exceed the amount of any gain or
18 economic benefit that the person derived from the violation, or up to $10,000 for each offense,
19 whichever amount is greater. Each violation of unlicensed or unlawful practice shall be deemed a
20 separate offense. The board, the state's attorney general, or a prosecuting attorney of any county or
21 municipality where the act of unlawful practice takes place may maintain an action to enjoin any
22 person or entity from continuing to do acts of unlawful practice. The action to enjoin shall not
23 replace any other civil, criminal, or regulatory remedy. An injunction without bond is available to
24 anyboard.
25 II. No individual, corporation, or other entity or person may provide, advertise, or
26 otherwise offer services requiring professional licensure to the public in this state, or
27 otherwise engage in unlawful practice as described in paragraph I through Internet-based
28 artificial intelligence, unless those services are delivered by a licensed professional. For
29 purposes of this section, artificial intelligence means any computer-based system, software,
30 or algorithm designed to perform tasks that typically require human cognitive functions,
