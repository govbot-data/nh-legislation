AMENDEDANALYSIS
This bill grants immunity from civil liability to farmers for damages resulting from claims based
on harms caused by certain per and poly fluorinated alkyl substances present in soil, water, or
agricultural products. This bill also requires the department of environmental services to develop
concentration-basedstandardsforPFASinbiosolidsatland-applicationsites.
