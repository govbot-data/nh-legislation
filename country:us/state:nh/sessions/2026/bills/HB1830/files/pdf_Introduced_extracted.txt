METHODOLOGY:
Thisbill:
 Requires the Department of Education, in consultation with the Department of Safety
andqualifiedfirearmsinstructors,todeveloporadoptstandardizedK–12firearmssafety
training by January 1, 2027, and to provide curricular materials to school districts at no
cost.
 Directs the Department of Education to seek federal and state grants and other funding
sources, and to establish guidelines for accepting private donations to support firearms
safetytraining.
 Mandates that beginning in the 2027 - 2028 school year, public schools provide at least
one hour of annual, age-appropriate firearms safety instruction as part of the health,
physical education, or civics curriculum, unless aparent or legal guardian opts a student
out.
o Specifies that instruction for grades K–5 focus on avoidance and reporting, and
for grades 6–12 include firearm mechanics, safe handling, secure storage, and an

overview of state and federal firearms laws, without the use of live firearms or
ammunition.
 Allows instruction to be delivered by school staff or certified external firearms safety
instructorsapprovedbytheDepartmentofEducation.
 Requiresschooldistrictstoadoptanopt-outpolicyforparentsorlegalguardians.
 Requires annual reporting by school districts to the Department of Education on
compliance and participation, and a consolidated statewide report to legislative
leadershipbeginningin2028.
