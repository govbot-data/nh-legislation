METHODOLOGY:
Thisbillmakesvariouschanges,relatedtotheFishandGameDepartment:
Section1(Effective60DaysAfterPassage)
 Authorizes the Fish and Game Department, through rulemaking under RSA 541-A, to
establishapermitorfeestructuretorecoverthecostsofprovidingpersonnel,equipment,
orspecializedexpertisetootherstateagenciesordepartments.
o The Department states it cannot project the frequency with which other agencies
would request such assistance or the level at which fees would be set.
Accordingly, any resulting revenue to the Department and related expenditures,
bothfortheDepartmentandforrequestingagencies,areindeterminable.
Section2and3(EffectiveJuly1,2026)
 Expandseligibilityforthepurchaseofadiscountednewbornlifetimehuntingandfishing
license by allowing the license to be sold from the date of birth through the child’s first
birthday,ratherthanonlyuptothedaybeforethefirstbirthdayundercurrentlaw.
o The Department states it cannot reasonably estimate how many parents would
choose to purchase a lifetime license on a child’s first birthday. Accordingly, any
changeinrevenueresultingfromthisprovisionisindeterminable.

 Provides that interest earned on the prepaid Fish and Game License Fund in excess of
five percent (5%) be treated as unrestricted funds, available for expenditure for any
lawfulpurposeauthorizedunderRSA206:38.
o The Department states this provision would not change the amount of interest
earned, but would change the classification of certain interest earnings from
restricted to unrestricted. As a result, such funds would become available for
general departmental use, and the Department assumes any unrestricted funds
realizedunderthisprovisioncouldbeexpendedasitbecomesavailable.
 Establishes a separate, nonlapsing Fish and Game Modernization and Sustainability
Account (“Account”) within the Fish and Game Fund, distinct from existing transfers
under RSA 214:9-c, IV and V. The bill allows up to $600,000 of principal from the
prepaid Fish and Game License Fund to be credited to the account in each of FY 2027
and FY 2028. Beginning in FY 2029, it also provides for a continual annual
appropriation of $50,000, credited in a lump sum, provided the prepaid license fund
balance is not reduced below $1,000,000. This account would be continually
appropriatedtotheDepartment.
o The Department states the Account would support technology and operational
modernization initiatives, including implementation of a customer relationship
management system intended to improve customer service, increase operational
efficiency, and support long-term revenue growth. The Department assumes the
$600,000 transfers in FY 2027 and FY 2028 would be fully expended in those
fiscal years. While the Department cannot quantify the precise revenue gains
expected from modernization, it anticipates substantial long-term benefits from
improved efficiency and accessibility, noting that comparable technology
investmentstypicallyrecovertheircostswithinapproximately12to18months.
It should be noted this bill does continually appropriate this new Account to the Department,
therefore, the Department would not need to request the funds be included in the operating
budget or need to come before the Fiscal Committee and Governor and Council to request to
acceptandbudgetfundsfromthisAccount.
