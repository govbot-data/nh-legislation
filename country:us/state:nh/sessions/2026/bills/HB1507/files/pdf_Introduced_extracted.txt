ANALYSIS
This bill requires public school districts, school administrative units (SAUs), and public charter
school boards to develop a policy requiring a minimum amount of recess time per day. This bill also
qualifies recess as instructional time in the school day, and provides districts, SAUs, and charter
schoolboardstheauthoritytoalterrequiredrecesstimeincertaincircumstances.
