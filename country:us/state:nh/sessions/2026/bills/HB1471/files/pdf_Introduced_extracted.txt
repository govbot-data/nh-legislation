ANALYSIS
This bill updates the calculation of “average final compensation” for group II retirement system
members (police and fire) who began service before July 1, 2011 and were not vested by January 1,
2012,aligningitwiththeformulaadoptedinHB2(2025).

FISCALIMPACT:
TheOfficeofLegislativeBudgetAssistantandtheNewHampshireRetirementSystem(NHRS)
state this bill is aligning the formula for the calculation of average final compensation for
Group II, Tier B members who commenced service before July 1, 2011 and were not vested by
January 1, 2012 with the provisions previously adopted in Chapter 142, Laws of 2025 (HB 282)
and therefore has no additional fiscal impact on state, county and local expenditures or
revenue. The anticipated fiscal impact of this calculation has already been considered as part
oftheappropriationcontainedinChapter142:41,Lawsof2025(HB282,section41).
