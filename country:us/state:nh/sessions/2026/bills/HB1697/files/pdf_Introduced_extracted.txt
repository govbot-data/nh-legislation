ANACT exemptingcertainfirearms and firearm accessories manufactured and retained in

BeitEnactedbytheSenateandHouseofRepresentativesinGeneralCourtconvened:
1 1 ShortTitle. Thisactmaybeknownandcitedasthe"FirearmsFreedomandFederalismAct."
2 2 New Chapter; In-State Only Firearms Usage. Amend RSA by inserting after chapter 159-F
3 thefollowingnewchapter:
4 CHAPTER159-G
5 IN-STATEONLYFIREARMSUSAGE
6 159-G:1 Firearms and Accessories Exempt from Commerce Clause. Any firearm or firearm
7 accessory manufactured in this state, including rifles, pistols, revolvers, shotguns, slides, receivers,
8 barrels, suppressors, magazines, grips, and stocks, may be marked "For N.H. Use Only. Export is
9 Criminal." Marked firearms and accessories shall not be possessed or exported outside this state
10 andshallbeexemptfrominterstatecommerce.
11 159-G:2 CriminalPenalties.
12 I. It shall be a class B misdemeanor to export or transfer a marked firearm or firearm
13 accessoryunderRSA159-G:1outsidethebordersofthisstate.
14 II. It shall be a class B misdemeanor to alter, destroy, or remove "For N.H. Use Only.
15 ExportisCriminal"markingfromfirearmsorfirearmsaccessories,unlessthefirearmoraccessoryis
16 renderedpermanentlyinoperable.
17 159-G:3 SerialNumberandManufacturingCertificate.
18 I. Each marked firearm or firearm accessory shall include a unique serial number inscribed
19 thereon,consistingofthefirstthreelettersofthecity,town,orunincorporatedplaceofmanufacture,
20 followed by the two-digit month, two-digit day, and two-digit year of manufacture, and adding
21 sequentialadditionaldigitsforfurtheritemsmanufacturedonthesameday.
22 II. The manufacturer shall issue a certificate of manufacture bearing the same serial
23 number, which a purchaser shall maintain for the duration of possession. The certificate shall
24 include multiple lines for recording transfers of ownership, including dates and transferee
25 information.
26 159-G:4 Federal Regulation Exemption. Firearms and firearms accessories marked "For N.H.
27 Use Only. Export is Criminal," that are located within this state shall be ineligible for federal
28 regulation under Article I, Section 8, Clause 3 of the U.S. Constitution and any act of the United
29 States Congress reliant on authority delegated under the Commerce Clause of the United States
30 Constitution.
