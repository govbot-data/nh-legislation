AMENDEDANALYSIS
Thisbill:
I. Modifies how insurance companies can claim tax credits for assessments paid to the New
Hampshire Life and Health Insurance Guaranty Association by providing that, for certain assessments
made after the effective date of the act, a member insurer may reduce its tax liability by claiming 10
percentoftheassessmentperyearfor10consecutiveyears.
II. Exempts conduct of a sports book or participation in sports wagering from criminal gambling
statutesincertaininstances.
