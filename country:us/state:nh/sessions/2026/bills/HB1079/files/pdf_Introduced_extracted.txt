ANACT allowing accessory dwelling units to be built within or attached to certain non-
conformingstructures.
SPONSORS: Rep.Howland,Straf.20;Rep.D.Paige,Carr.1;Rep.Preece,Hills.17

ANALYSIS
This bill expands the definition of accessory dwelling units to allow them to be built within
nonconformingstructures.
