ANACT relative to the suspension period in cases involving the administrative appeals
unit relative to licensure or certification concerning lead paint poisoning
prevention.

Hills.32;Rep.DeRoy,Straf.3

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ANALYSIS
This bill allows for individuals or educational programs licensed or certified to perform various
lead abatement, inspection, or educational functions to reapply for a license or certificate 2 years
aftertheirinitialnoticeofrevocation.
