METHODOLOGY:
This bill, effective July 1, 2026 (for the 2026-2027 school year), expands eligibility for free or
reduced-price school breakfasts and lunches to students who are age 21 if they were eligible for
the federal meal programs at age 20. These students remain eligible until they turn 22,
graduate, withdraw, or complete their educational program. School districts and chartered
public schools may receive federal reimbursement first, with state reimbursement available only
for meals not eligible for federal funding. The Department of Education has provided
assumptionsandanestimateastothisbill’spotentialfiscalimpact:
