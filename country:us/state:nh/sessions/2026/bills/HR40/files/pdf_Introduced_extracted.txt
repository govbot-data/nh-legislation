IntheYearofOurLordTwoThousandTwenty-Six
ARESOLUTION urgingthelegislaturetoadequatelyfundpubliceducation.
1 Whereas, in its 1993 and 1997 decisions, in the Claremont series of public school funding cases,
2 the New Hampshire Supreme Court ruled that the New Hampshire Constitution imposes upon the
3 legislature 4 obligations with respect to public K-12 education: (1) define an adequate education; (2)
4 determine its cost; (3) fund it with constitutional taxes; and (4) ensure its delivery through
5 accountability;and
6 Whereas, while the legislature has unjustifiably had reservations as to the court's authority to
7 impose obligations upon a coordinate branch of government, the legislature, in the spirit of comity,
8 hasdevotedextensivetime,energyandpublictaxdollarstosatisfyingthem;and
9 Whereas, in the most recent of these cases, the judicial branch correctly asserted authority to
10 review the legislature's determinations with respect to its 4 supposed obligations, and to fund
11 educationatlevelsdeterminedbythecourt;and
12 Whereas, the legislature now deems it necessary to definitively proclaim that the legislature
13 shallconformtotheholdingsanddeterminationsofthestate'ssupremejudicialauthorityastowhat
14 thestate'seducationfundingpoliciesshallbe;now,therefore,beit
15 ResolvedbytheHouseofRepresentatives:
16 That the general court has the responsibility and obligation to adequately fund a student's
17 education;and
18 That the general court ought to comply with the judicial branch's findings in the most recent
19 Claremontcase.
