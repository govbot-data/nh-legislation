4 RSA 21-P:42-a shall be entitled to immunity from prosecution as a result of the repeal of RSA 21-
5 P:42-a.

7 orordersissuedduringtheCOVID-19stateofemergency,isrepealed.

9 aftertheeffectivedateofthissection,andthisrepealshallnotbeconstruedtoremovetheimmunity
10 from liability for any person or entity that was entitled to such immunity prior to the effective date
11 ofthisact.
12 4 EffectiveDate. Thisactshalltakeeffectuponitspassage.
