HOUSEBILL
ANACT relative to exemptions from multi-family zoning requirements for small or low-
densitycommunities.
SPONSORS: Rep.Meuse,Rock.37;Rep.Mandelbaum,Rock.21
COMMITTEE: Housing
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ANALYSIS
This bill exempts municipalities with fewer than 250 residents per square mile from the
statutory requirement to permit multi-family residential development on commercially zoned land,
regardlessoffederalruralorurbandesignationsoroverlappingareaclassifications.
