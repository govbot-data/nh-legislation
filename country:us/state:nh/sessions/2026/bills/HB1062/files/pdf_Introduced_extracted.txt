IntheYearofOurLordTwoThousandTwenty-Six
ANACT authorizing the secretary of state to conduct random audits of the citizenship
qualificationofregisteredvoters.
BeitEnactedbytheSenateandHouseofRepresentativesinGeneralCourtconvened:
1 1 New Section; Random Citizenship Audits. Amend RSA 654 by inserting after section 45 the
2 followingnewsection:
3 654:45-a RandomCitizenshipAudits.
4 I. The secretary of state may conduct random audits of the citizenship qualifications of
5 registered voters and, for this purpose, may use any and all available governmental and private
6 databases to confirm the citizenship of voters who submitted a qualified voter affidavit as proof of
7 citizenship.
8 II. Where the citizenship of a voter is confirmed through audit, such confirmation shall be
9 noted in the statewide voter registration database, and that voter shall not be subject to further
10 auditofhisorhercitizenship.
11 III. If the audits reveal information of any potential non-citizens on the checklist, such
12 informationshallbesubmittedtotheattorneygeneralforfurtherreviewandappropriateaction.
13 2 EffectiveDate. Thisactshalltakeeffect60daysafteritspassage.
