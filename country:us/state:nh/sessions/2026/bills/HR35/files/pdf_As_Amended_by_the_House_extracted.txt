1 Whereas, many atmospheric activities such as weather modification, stratospheric aerosol
2 injection (SAI), solar radiation modification (SRM), and other forms of geoengineering, involving the
3 intentional release of polluting emissions, harm human health and safety, the environment,

5 Whereas, it is the intention of the New Hampshire house of representatives to prohibit the
6 intentional injection, release, or dispersion, by any means, of chemicals, chemical compounds,
7 substances, or apparatus within the borders of this state into the atmosphere with the express
8 purpose of affecting temperature, weather, or the intensity of the sunlight, thereby prohibiting
9 geoengineering (including SAI and SRM) and other intentionally polluting activities in New
10 Hampshire’s atmosphere and/or at ground level to preserve the safe, healthful, and peaceful uses of
11 NewHampshire’satmosphereforpeople,wildlife,andagriculture;and

19 bordersofthestateofNewHampshire.
