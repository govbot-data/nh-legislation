5 UOCAVAVoterCitizenshipVerification
6 657:27 Purpose. The purpose of this act is to ensure the integrity of the electoral process by
7 requiring Uniformed and Overseas Citizens Absentee Voting Act (UOCAVA) voters, except military
8 members and their families, to submit proof of United States citizenship one time to be eligible to
9 voteinNewHampshireelections,effectiveJanuary1,2026.
10 657:28 Definitions. Inthissubdivision:
11 I. "UOCAVA voter" means an individual eligible to vote in New Hampshire under the
12 UniformedandOverseasCitizensAbsenteeVotingAct(52U.S.C.sections20301–20311).
13 II. "Military member" means an individual serving as a member of the uniformed services,
14 as defined in 52 U.S.C. section 20310, including active duty and reserve components of the Army,
15 Navy,AirForce,MarineCorps,CoastGuard,orSpaceForce.
16 III. "Family of a military member" means the spouse or dependent of a military member, as
17 definedin52U.S.C.section20310.
18 IV. "Proof of citizenship" means a valid document establishing United States citizenship,
19 including but not limited to a United States passport, birth certificate issued by a United States
20 stateorterritory,CertificateofCitizenship,orNaturalizationCertificate.
21 V. "Secretary of state" refers to the New Hampshire secretary of state or their designated
22 representative.
23 657:29 CitizenshipVerificationRequirementforUOCAVAVoters.
24 I. Beginning January 1, 2027, all UOCAVA voters, except military members and their
25 families, seeking to register or maintain voter registration in New Hampshire shall submit proof of
26 citizenshiponetimetothesecretaryofstate.
27 II. Military members and their families shall be exempt from the proof of citizenship
28 requirementunderthissubdivision.
29 III. The secretary of state shall accept the following as proof of citizenship for non-exempt
30 UOCAVAvoters:
