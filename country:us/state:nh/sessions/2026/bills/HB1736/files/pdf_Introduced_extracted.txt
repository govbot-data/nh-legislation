HOUSEBILL
ANACT repealing the authority of the public utilities commission to approve alternative
formsofregulationforutilities.
SPONSORS: Rep.Sweeney,Rock.25
COMMITTEE: Science,TechnologyandEnergy
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ANALYSIS
This bill repeals the public utilities commission authority to approve and implement alternative
forms of regulation for investor-owned electric and gas utilities outside of traditional cost-of-service,
ratebase,andrateofreturnmethods.
