ANALYSIS
This bill expands qualifications for initial licensure as an alcohol and drug counselor (LADC).
The bill permits an individual who is credentialed as an internationally certified alcohol and drug
counselorandwhohassupervisedworkexperiencetoqualifyforLADClicensure.
