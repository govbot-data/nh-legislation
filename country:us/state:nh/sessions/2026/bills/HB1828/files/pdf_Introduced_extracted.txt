─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ANALYSIS
This bill requires the department of education to review professional educator preparation
programs to ensure effective literacy instruction is provided in institutions of higher education. The
bill also authorizes the department to make rules relative to the auditing process of preparation
programs.

4 I. The department of education shall review New Hampshire approved professional
5 preparationprogramsto:
6 (a) Ensure that all educators complete coursework in evidence-based strategies for
