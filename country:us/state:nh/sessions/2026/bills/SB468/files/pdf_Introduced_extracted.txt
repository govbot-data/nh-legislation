4 XI. Each alternative treatment center registered under this section may request
5 authorizationtooperateagreenhousecultivationlocation,atthesameoratadifferentlocationthan
6 its existing cultivation location, in order to reduce energy costs and provide lower prices for
7 registeredqualifyingpatients.
8 (a) Thedepartmentshalladoptrules,pursuanttoRSA541-A,which:
9 (1) Allow alternative treatment centers to apply for authorization to conduct
10 greenhousecultivation;and
11 (2) Arenecessarytoregulatetheoperationofgreenhousecultivation,includingrules
12 regardinglocationandsecurity.
13 (b) An alternative treatment center requesting authorization to operate a greenhouse
14 cultivation location shall submit a detailed proposal to the department, pursuant to rules adopted
15 under this section and RSA 541-A, which shall include the alternative treatment center’s plan to
16 reduceenergycostsandprovidelowerpricesforregisteredqualifyingpatients.
17 (c) A greenhouse cultivation location shall also be subject to rules adopted by the
18 department under RSA 126-X:6, III, and all applicable provisions of this chapter, including, but not
19 limitedto,compliancewithlocalzoninglaws.
20 (d) The department shall, in conjunction with the local governing body of the town or
21 city where the greenhouse cultivation location would be located, solicit input from qualifying
22 patients,designatedcaregivers,andresidentsofthetownorcityinwhichthegreenhousecultivation
23 locationwouldbelocated.
24 (e) Any alternative treatment center which operates a greenhouse shall include in its
25 annual report to the department, required by RSA 126-X:8, XII, the impacts of greenhouse
26 cultivation on energy costs and product prices. The department shall include this information in its
27 annualreportrequiredunderRSA126-X:10,V.
