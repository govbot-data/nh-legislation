HOUSEBILL
ANACT requiring scholarship organizations to provide written notice to parents
explaining the parents' requirement to provide written notice upon termination of
ahomeeducationprogram.
SPONSORS: Rep. Peternel, Carr. 6; Rep. Bennett, Rock. 4; Rep. Burnham, Straf. 2; Rep. Drye,
Sull. 7; Rep. Granger, Straf. 2; Rep. Layon, Rock. 13; Rep. Noble, Hills. 2; Rep.
Thibault,Merr.25
COMMITTEE: EducationPolicyandAdministration
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ANALYSIS
This bill requires scholarship organizations to provide a written explanation to parents of
students that have been receiving a home education of the parents' requirement to provide written
notice to the district superintendent or school principal upon termination of a home education
program.
