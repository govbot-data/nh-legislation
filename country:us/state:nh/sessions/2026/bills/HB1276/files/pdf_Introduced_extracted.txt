BeitEnactedbytheSenateandHouseofRepresentativesinGeneralCourtconvened:
1 1 New Section; Liability for Death of a Companion Animal. Amend RSA 508 by inserting after
2 section22thefollowingnewsection:
3 508:23 LiabilityforDeathofaCompanionAnimal.
4 I. A person shall be liable for up to $5,000 in non-economic damages to the owner of a
5 companion animal upon the death of a companion animal if such person causes the death of such
6 companion animal through intentional or negligent conduct. If the death was caused by a negligent
7 act, such act shall have occurred on the property of the owner of the companion animal while the
8 companion animal was under the control of its owner or other caretaker. Noneconomic damages
9 awarded pursuant to this section are limited to compensation for the loss of the reasonably expected
10 companionship,love,andaffectionofapet.
11 II. ThelimitationinparagraphIshallnotapplytocausesofactionforintentionalemotional
12 distress or any other civil action other than the cause of action involving the direct and sole loss of a
13 companionanimal.
14 III. Thisactionshallnot:
15 (a) Apply to a nonprofit entity or governmental agency, or its employees, negligently
16 causingthedeathofapetwhileactingonbehalfofpublichealthoranimalwelfare.
17 (b) Authorize an award of noneconomic damages in an action for professional negligence
18 againstalicensedveterinarian.
19 (c) Applytothekillingofanydogasauthorizedunderothertitles.
20 2 EffectiveDate. ThisactshalltakeeffectJanuary1,2027.
