AMENDEDANALYSIS
Thisbill:
I. Provides that vaccination requirements for adults residing in foster homes shall not exceed the
vaccination requirements of RSA 141-C:20-a, either in type of vaccination, quality of doses, or
authorizationforexemption.
II. Removes the resource limit for the Medicare savings program and, subject to CMS approval,
makes the low income subsidy for Medicare Part D available to residents to assist with prescription drug
costs.
