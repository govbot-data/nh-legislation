AMENDEDANALYSIS
This bill makes the budget committee responsible for the determination of the default budget in
any local political subdivision which has adopted the official ballot referenda form of meeting
pursuanttoRSA40:14andhasalsoadoptedamunicipalbudgetcommitteepursuanttoRSA32:14.
