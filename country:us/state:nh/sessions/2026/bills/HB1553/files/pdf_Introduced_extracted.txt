1 1 New Chapter; Pet Fees in Rental Housing. Amend RSA by inserting after chapter 540-C the
2 followingnewchapter:
3 CHAPTER540-D
4 PETFEESINRENTALHOUSING
5 540-D:1 Purpose. The purpose of this chapter is to protect tenants and promote housing
6 stabilityby:
7 I. Limitingmonthlypetrenttoareasonableandpredictableamount.
8 II. Eliminating nonrefundable pet fees that create financial barriers for renters with
9 companionanimals.
10 III. Ensuring that landlords retain the ability to protect property while preventing punitive
11 orexcessivechargesthatforcetenantstogiveuppets.
12 540-D:2 Cap on Pet Rent. A landlord shall not demand or receive additional rent from a tenant
13 as a condition of allowing a pet to reside at the residence in an amount that exceeds one percent of
14 themonthlyrentofthedwellingunit,regardlessofthenumberofpetskeptintheunit.
15 540-D:3 Prohibition on Nonrefundable Fees. A landlord shall not demand or receive any
16 additionalsecuritydeposit beyondthat allowedunder RSA540-A:6. Nonrefundablepet feesshall be
17 prohibitedunderthischapter.
18 540-D:4 Exceptions.
19 I. Thischaptershallnotapplytoserviceanimalsorsupportanimalsprotectedunderfederal
20 orstatedisabilitylaw.
21 II. Nothinginthischaptershallpreventalandlordfromenforcingleaseprovisionsrequiring
22 tenantstorepairorpayforactualdamagecausedbyapet.
23 540-D:5 Enforcement. A tenant may bring an action in court for violations of this chapter and
24 mayrecoveractualdamages,plusreasonableattorney’sfeesandcourtcosts.
25 2 EffectiveDate. Thisactshalltakeeffect60daysafteritspassage.
