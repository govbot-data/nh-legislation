3 433-A:13 PlantRegulatorandBiostimulants.
4 I. Inthissection:
5 (a) "Plant regulator" means any substance or mixture of substances intended, through
6 physiological action, for accelerating or retarding the rate of growth or rate of maturation, or for
7 otherwisealteringthebehavior,ofplantsortheproducethereof. "Plantregulator"doesnotinclude:
8 (1) Substances to the extent that they are intended as plant nutrients, trace
9 elements,nutritionalchemicals,plantinoculants,soilamendments,orvitaminhormoneproducts;or
10 (2) Plantbiostimulantsthat:
11 (A) Areofbiologicalorigin;or
12 (B) Include chemical compounds that are synthetically derived, but are
13 structurallysimilarandfunctionallyidenticalto,substancesofbiologicalorigin.
14 (b) "Plant biostimulant" means a substance, microorganism, or mixture thereof, that,
15 when applied to seeds, plants, the rhizosphere, soil, or other growth media, act to support a plant’s
16 natural processes independently of the nutrient content of that substance, microorganism, or
17 mixturethereof,andthattherebyimproves:
18 (1) Nutrientavailability,uptake,oruseefficiency;
19 (2) Tolerancetoabioticstress;and
20 (3) Consequentgrowth,development,quality,oryield.
21 (c) "Nutritionalchemical":
22 (1) Means any substance or mixture of substances that interacts with plant
23 nutrientsinamannerwhichimprovesnutrientavailabilityoraidstheplantinacquiringorutilizing
24 plantnutrients;and
25 (2) Includessomeplantbiostimulants.
26 (d) "Vitamin hormone product" means a product consisting of a mixture of plant
27 hormones,plantnutrients,inoculants,orsoilamendments.
28 II. No later than 120 days after the date of the enactment of this section, the commissioner
29 shall make rules under RSA 541-A relative to the sale and use of plant regulators, plant
30 biostimulants,nutritionalchemicals,andvitaminhormoneproducts.
