1 1 Weather Modification; Emergency Cloud Seeding. RSA 12-F:1 is repealed and reenacted to
2 readasfollows:
3 I. Cloud seeding may only occur in New Hampshire if the state is determined to be in
4 extreme or exceptional drought as categorized by the United States Drought Monitor for at least 90
5 days.
6 II. Prior to engaging in cloud seeding, the entity seeking to conduct the activity shall
7 conduct an environmental review, assessing potential risks and benefits, and ensuring that the
8 activities will not result in harm to the environment, public health, or wildlife. The review shall be
9 submitted to the department of environmental services. The department may reject the activity if
10 the environmental review demonstrates, or the department determines, the activity will result in
11 harmtotheenvironment,publichealth,orwildlife.
12 III. At least one month prior to any cloud seeding activities the department of
13 environmentalservicesshalldothefollowing:
14 (a) Place a public notice in at least 4 newspapers within the state and on the
15 departmental internet website informing the public of the date and type of the proposed cloud
16 seedingactivitieswithinthestate.
17 (b) Disclose to the public the chemical compounds and technologies to be used in the
18 proposed activities in the state, including the effects of the program on human health, what wildlife
19 andwildlifehabitatsmaybeaffected,andthesourceoffundingtoperformsuchactivities.
20 IV. On April 1of eachcalendar year, thedepartment of environmental services shall submit
21 a report to the governor, speaker of the house, and senate president detailing any cloud seeding
22 activities that occurred the prior calendar year. If no activities under this chapter occurred in the
23 priorcalendaryear,areportisnotrequiredtobesubmitted.
