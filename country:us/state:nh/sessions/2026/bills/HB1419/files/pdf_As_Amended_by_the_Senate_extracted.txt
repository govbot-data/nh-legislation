14 potentialadoptionofprovisionsmodeledafter,orimprovingupon,Delawaregeneralcorporationlaw;
15 (c) Evaluate mechanisms for recruiting, appointing, and retaining chancellors and vice
16 chancellors with deep corporate law expertise, including term lengths, compensation, and
17 appointmentprocesses;
18 (d) Investigate partnerships with the university of New Hampshire Franklin Pierce
19 school of law, or other local and regional institutions, to create clerkship pipelines, research centers,

22 related incorporation services, and recommend strategies to make New Hampshire attractive for
