METHODOLOGY:
This bill repeals the authority for municipalities to impose and collect motor vehicle registration
permit fees, however, it maintains RSA 261:154, which allows governing bodies of towns and
cities with a population greater than 50,000, as determined by the last federal census, to assess
additional fees for registration permits over and above the fee schedule in RSA 261:153. Nashua
and Manchester are the only two municipalities with the ability to use RSA 261:154. The
DepartmentofSafety,forinformationalpurposes,hasprovidedthefollowingtotalsformunicipal
motorvehicleregistrationfeescollectedinFY2025:
FY2025TotalMVFees(LocalPortion)
Total-AllMunicipalities $346,101,214
Manchester $23,343,814

Nashua $18,186,552
To implement the requirements of this bill, the Department of Safety states it would need to
make comprehensive updates to its registration database, MAAP, certify all affected third-party
software vendors, and revise workflows across all vehicle registration processes. These system
modifications and vendor certifications would require coordinated development, testing, and
deployment to ensure compliance with the new provisions. Based on current estimates, the
Department anticipates a one-time cost of approximately $140,000 to complete these updates
andimplementthenecessaryregistration-relatedchanges.
Itshouldalsobenotedthatbillsamendingmotorvehicleregistration,title,ordriverlicenselaws
typicallyareeffectiveonJanuary1. Thisbilliseffective60daysafterpassage.
