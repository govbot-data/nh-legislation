ANALYSIS
This bill defines child care centers for purposes of local zoning requirements and provides that,
with limited exception, municipalities shall allow the operation of a child care center by right on
commercially zoned land provided that the center is in compliance with child care licensing
requirementsofthedepartmentofhealthandhumanservices.
