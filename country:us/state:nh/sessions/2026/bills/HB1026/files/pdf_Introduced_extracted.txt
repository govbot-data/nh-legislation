ANALYSIS
This bill clarifies that municipalities may prohibit accessory dwelling units with manufactured
housing,anditspecifiesthatmanufacturedhousingdoesnotincludeaccessorydwellingunits.

19 2 ManufacturedHousingDefinition. AmendRSA674:31toreadasfollows:
20 674:31 Definition. As used in this subdivision, "manufactured housing" means any structure,
21 transportable in one or more sections, which, in the traveling mode, is 8 body feet or more in width
22 and 40 body feet or more in length, or when erected on site, is 320 square feet or more, and which is
