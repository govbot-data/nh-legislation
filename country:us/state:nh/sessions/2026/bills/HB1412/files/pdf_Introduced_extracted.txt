─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ANALYSIS
This bill adds requirements for the display of flags in schools and adds public charter schools as
well as public academies to the statute. The bill also permits certain private schools to opt out of
saidrequirementsandauthorizeslocalschoolboardstoacceptmonetarydonationsforthepurposeof
acquiringflagsandrelatedappliances.
