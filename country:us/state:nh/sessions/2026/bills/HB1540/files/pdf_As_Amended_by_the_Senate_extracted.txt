AMENDEDANALYSIS
Thisbill:
I. Clarifies that municipalities retain authority to enforce local health ordinances related to
septicandmaintenancewhenregulatingaccessorydwellingunits.
II. Implements an increase which separates the on-premises license for barbershops and salons
into two tiers, one which allows for a single sale and one which allows for service without charge,
and requires the liquor commission to make an annual report to the legislature regarding the
issuanceoflicensesandthenumberofviolationswhichoccurred.
