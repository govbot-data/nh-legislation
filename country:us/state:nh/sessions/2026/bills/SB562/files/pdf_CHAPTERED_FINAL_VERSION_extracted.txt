ANALYSIS
This bill establishes the Granite State home mitigation and resiliency program to provide financial
grants to homeowners to mitigate residential real property against loss from severe weather events. The
billalsoestablishesafundtoprovidesupportforthisprogram.
Thisbillisarequestoftheinsurancedepartment.

8 407-E:2 Purpose and Policy. The purpose of this chapter is to establish a program to provide
9 financial grants to homeowners to mitigate residential real property against loss from severe weather
10 events,includingbutnotlimitedtothoseinvolvingrain,wind,snow,ice,sleet,hail,floods,seawater,lake
11 water, river water, surface water, tornadoes, hurricanes, and microbursts. The intent of this chapter is to
12 provide New Hampshire residents with the opportunity to mitigate risks to their homes from severe
13 weather events, thereby reducing claim severity, frequency of claims, and insurance premiums for New
14 Hampshire homeowners. This chapter does not create an entitlement for property owners and does not
15 obligate the state to pay for the residential real property to be inspected, constructed, improved,
16 remodeled, mitigated, retrofitted, or otherwise modified. The granite state home mitigation and resiliency
17 program is subject to the availability of funds in the granite state home mitigation and resiliency fund
18 establishedinRSA407-E:4.
