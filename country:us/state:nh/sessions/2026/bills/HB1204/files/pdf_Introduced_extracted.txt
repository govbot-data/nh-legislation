1 1 New Section; Eligibility of New Hampshire Lumber for SPF Grading. Amend RSA 426 by
2 insertingaftersection18thefollowingnewsection:
3 426:18-a EligibilityforSPFGrading.
4 Notwithstanding any provision of law or rule to the contrary, softwood timber harvested in New
5 Hampshire at or above 44 degrees North latitude shall be eligible for classification and grading as
6 spruce-pine-fir(SPF),providedthat:
7 I. The timber is of a species customarily included in the SPF grouping under the American
8 SoftwoodLumberStandard(PS20);and
9 II. The timber meets all applicable structural and visual grading requirements for SPF as
10 established by a recognized grading agency accredited by the American Lumber Standard
11 Committee.

13 RequirementsforStateFundedBuildings. AmendRSA155-A:13byinsertingafterparagraphIIthe
14 followingnewparagraph:
15 III. The material specification of any state-funded building in New Hampshire shall give
16 preference to lumber harvested in New Hampshire, unless design criteria necessitates utilizing
17 importedsourcing.
18 3 EffectiveDate. Thisactshalltakeeffect60daysafteritspassage.
