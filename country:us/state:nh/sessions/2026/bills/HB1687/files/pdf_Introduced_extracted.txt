HOUSEBILL
ANACT removing the power of courts within the judicial branch family division to issue
criminaldeterminations,judgments,andpenalties.
SPONSORS: Rep.Love,Rock.13
COMMITTEE: ChildrenandFamilyLaw
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ANALYSIS
This bill restricts the jurisdiction of courts within the judicial branch family division to not be
able to issue any criminal determinations, judgments, or penalties relative to matters which they
maypresideover.
