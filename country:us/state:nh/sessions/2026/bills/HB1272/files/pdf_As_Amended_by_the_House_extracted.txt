1 1 New Section; Party Affiliation on the Official Ballot; Ballot Systems; Town Elections. Amend
2 RSA669byinsertingaftersection13thefollowingnewsection:
3 669:13-a PartyAffiliationontheOfficialBallot.
4 I. Anytownthat hasadopted anofficial ballotsystem underRSA669:12or RSA669:13may
5 vote to include on the official ballot each candidate’s party affiliation after the candidate’s name,
6 unless waived by the candidate, under an article in the warrant for any town meeting. The town
7 clerk shall cause to be printed on the official ballot the question, "Shall the official ballot contain the
8 party affiliation of each candidate, appearing after each candidate’s name, pursuant to RSA 669:13-
9 a,I?”,whichquestionshallbefollowedbyboxesinwhichthevotermayindicate"Yes"or"No". Ifthe
10 majorityof thequalified voters present and voting at suchmeetingshall answer said questionin the
11 affirmative, the party affiliation of candidates shall be included on future official ballots, until
12 rescinded or until the unofficial ballot system is adopted. The party affiliation shall be the
13 candidate’s party affiliation listed on the town’s voter checklist at the time the candidate declares
14 candidacy and shall be denoted on the ballot after the candidate’s name using the first 3 letters of
15 the name of the party in parentheses or “(UND)” if candidate has no declared party. When filing for
16 office, a candidate may notify the town clerk that no party affiliation shall appear after their name
17 ontheofficialballot.
18 II. Any town that has adopted the provisions of RSA 669:13-a, I may vote to rescind these
19 provisions under an article in the warrant for any town meeting. The town clerk shall cause to be
20 printed on the official ballot the question, "Shall the official ballot no longer contain the party
21 affiliation of each candidate, pursuant to RSA 669:13-a, II?”, which question shall be followed by
22 boxes in which the voter may indicate "Yes" or "No". If the majority of the qualified voters present
23 and voting at such meeting shall answer said question in the affirmative, the party affiliation of
24 candidates shall no longer be included on future official ballots. The provisions of RSA 669:13-a, I
25 shallalsoberescindedifthetownadoptstheunofficialballotsystem.
