AMENDEDANALYSIS
Thisbill:
I. Replaces the state restriction on tip pooling and sharing with the federal Fair Labor
StandardsActrulesgoverningtippooling.
II. Authorizes the labor commissioner to adopt rules regarding the practice of tip pooling and
sharing.
III. Requires that establishments which require or permit employee participation in tip pooling
orsharingprovidenoticeofsuchpracticetopatrons.
