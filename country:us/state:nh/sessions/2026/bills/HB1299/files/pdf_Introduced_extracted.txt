─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ANALYSIS
This bill permits the public and private classification of individuals based on biological sex in
multi-user facilities, athletic and sporting competitions, and correctional and treatment facilities.
The bill also establishes that these forms of separation based on biological sex do not qualify as
discrimination.
