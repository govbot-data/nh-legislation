BeitEnactedbytheSenateandHouseofRepresentativesinGeneralCourtconvened:
1 1 Executive Councilor; State Senator; Vacancies. RSA 661:8 is repealed and reenacted to read
2 asfollows:
3 661:8 ExecutiveCouncilor;StateSenator.
4 I. If a vacancy occurs in the office of executive councilor or state senator, it shall be filled as
5 providedinpartII,articles34and62ofthestateconstitution.
6 II. Within 21 days after proof of a vacancy or a request that a vacancy be filled, the
7 governor, in the case of an executive council vacancy, or the governor and council, in the case of any
8 other vacancy, shall declare that there shall be a special election, which shall be held as provided in
9 RSA655:81and82orasprovidedinRSA661:8,III.
10 III. No special election shall be held after March 15 of the second year of the biennium, as
11 determinedbyRSA655:81,I.
12 2 Delegate to State Party Convention; State Representative. Amend RSA 661:10 to read as
13 follows:
14 661:10 State Representative; Delegate to State Party Convention. If a vacancy occurs in the
15 officeofstaterepresentativeordelegatetoastatepartyconvention,thevacancyshallnotbefilled.
16 3 EffectiveDate. Thisactshalltakeeffect60daysafteritspassage.
