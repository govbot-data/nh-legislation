HB 454  - AS INTRODUCED  2025 SESSION	25-0910	11/09 HOUSE BILL	454 AN ACT	requiring that retailers of fuel blends with 15% ethanol also offer fuel blends with 10% or lower ethanol content. SPONSORS:	Rep. T. Walsh, Merr. 10; Rep. Vose, Rock. 5; Rep. Hill, Merr. 2; Rep. S. Smith, Sull. 3 COMMITTEE:	Commerce and Consumer Affairs ----------------------------------------------------------------- ANALYSIS 	This bill requires a fuel retailer to sell a fuel blend with 10% ethanol or less if they sell a fuel blend with 15% or more of ethanol. - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -  Explanation:	Matter added to current law appears in bold italics.		Matter removed from current law appears [in brackets and struckthrough.]		Matter which is either (a) all new or (b) repealed and reenacted appears in regular type.	25-0910	11/09 STATE OF NEW HAMPSHIRE In the Year of Our Lord Two Thousand Twenty Five AN ACT	requiring that retailers of fuel blends with 15% ethanol also offer fuel blends with 10% or lower ethanol content. Be it Enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court convened:  1  New Section; Sale of Ethanol Fuel Blends.  Amend RSA 339 by inserting after section 30-a the following new section:	339:30-b  Sale of 15% and 10% or Lower Ethanol Fuel Blends.	All retailers of ethanol fuel blends shall offer for sale at least one fuel blend with a percentage of 10% ethanol or lower, if such retailer offers for sale a fuel blend with a percentage of 15% ethanol or greater.  The retailer shall post the prices of such fuel blends as required in RSA 339:30-a. 2  Effective Date.  This act shall take effect July 1, 2025.
