METHODOLOGY:
This bill authorizes the creation of vanity license plates and decals that identify amateur radio
operators by their Federal Communications Commission (FCC) call signs or a lightning bolt
symbol. The Department of Safety states this would require system programming changes to
accommodate the new plate types and the associated eligibility verification process, at an
estimated one-time cost of approximately $25,000. Vanity plates are produced on-demand, and
the cost of plate production, materials, and distribution is covered by existing vanity and plate
fees.
Itshouldbenotedthisbill statesthattheDirectoroftheDivisionofMotorVehiclesisallowed to
set a fee for the plates “as approved by the New Hampshire house and senate ways and means
committee”. It is assumed this means approved separately by the House and Senate Ways and
Means Committees, which is a deviation from standard practice, as similar fees are established
instatuteorthroughadministrativerulemaking.
