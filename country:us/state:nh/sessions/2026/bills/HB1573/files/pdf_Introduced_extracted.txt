ANALYSIS
This bill permits excused absences for students that are participating in civic engagement
activities and career or technical education (CTE) activities so long as parental notice is provided to
schools and other requirements aremet. The bill alsodirects the stateboard of educationto develop
rulesandalternativeacademiccreditsrelativetoparticipationincivicorCTEactivities.
