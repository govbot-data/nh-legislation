─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ANALYSIS
This bill makes it a criminal offense to possess a firearm in violation of a court order requiring
the surrender of firearms, and also makes the offense a qualifying offense triggering the
presumption that the defendant is dangerous and may be denied bail, if certain other conditions are
met.
