COMMITTEE: Housing
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ANALYSIS
This bill clarifies the definition of inclusionary zoning, expands municipal options for promoting
affordable housing, and establishes a process for applicants to propose alternative compliance
subjecttoindependentreview.
