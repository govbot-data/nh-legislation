ANALYSIS
This bill authorizes 2 or more contiguous municipalities to enter into a compact establishing an
intermunicipal governing authority for the purpose of jointly providing one or more municipal
services.

1 1 New Chapter; Intermunicpal Compacts. Amend RSA by inserting after chapter 53-G the
2 followingnewchapter:
3 CHAPTER53-H
4 INTERMUNICPALCOMPACTS
5 53-H:1 Authority.
6 Any2ormorecontiguousmunicipalitiesmay,byconcurrentvoteoftheirlegislativebodies,enter
7 into a compact establishing an intermunicipal governing authority for the purpose of jointly
8 providing one or more municipal services, including but not limited to public safety, water and
9 sewer,solidwaste,housing,planning,transportation,orotherservicesauthorizedbylaw.
10 53-H:2 GoverningBody.
11 I. Each authority established under this chapter shall be governed by a board composed of
12 representatives appointed by and from the governing body of each member municipality, in the
13 mannerprovidedinthecompactagreement.
14 II. The board shall be a political subdivision of the state, exercising only those powers
15 expresslydelegatedtoitinthefoundingcompact.
16 53-H:3 Powers.
17 Anintermunicipalgoverningauthoritymay:
18 I. Employstaff,adoptbylaws,andenterintocontracts.
19 II. Acquire,hold,lease,anddisposeofproperty,realandpersonal.
20 III. Issue debt obligations, subject to approval of the legislative bodies of the member
21 municipalities.
22 IV. Prepareandadoptannualbudgetsfortheservicesitprovides.
23 V. Assessandcollectcontributionsfrommembermunicipalitiesasprovidedinthecompact.
24 VI. Applyforandreceivegrants,aid,andrevenuesfromstate,federal,orprivatesources.
25 VII. Exercise any other powers incidental to carrying out its purposes, consistent with the
26 compactandthischapter.
27 53-H:4 Finance.
28 I. Each member municipality shall appropriate funds for its share of the authority’s budget
29 inthesamemannerasothermunicipalappropriations.
30 II. No authority created under this chapter shall have independent taxing power unless
31 expresslygrantedbyanactofthelegislature.
