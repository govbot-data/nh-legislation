ANACT providing that an individual's use of therapeutic cannabis shall not disqualify the
individualfromthepurchase,ownership,orpossessionofafirearm.
SPONSORS: Rep.TomMannion,Hills.1;Rep.Giasson,Hills.29;Rep.Layon,Rock.13;Rep.H.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ANALYSIS
This bill provides that lawful participation in the state's therapeutic cannabis program shall not
disqualifyan individual exercising their constitutional right to purchase, possess, carry or transfer a
firearm.
