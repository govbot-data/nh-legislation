This change will impact state adequacy aid to school districts, as well as state payments for
charter schools and education freedom accounts (EFAs). Data for FY 2028 payments is not yet

available, therefore this bill’s impact cannot be estimated at this time. Using most current data
available, this bill will likely increase total state education trust fund expenditures by
approximately $27 million per year ($25 million for district adequacy and $1 million each for
charterschoolsandEFAs).
It should be noted that this bill’s effective date is “60 days after its passage”. To not affect FY
2027 education payment calculations, the effective date should be July 1, 2027, which would
meetthebill’sintentofhavingFY2028beingthefirstyearofimpact.
