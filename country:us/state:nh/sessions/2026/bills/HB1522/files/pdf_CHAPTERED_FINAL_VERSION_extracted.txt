ANALYSIS
Thisbill:
I. Adds to the types of abuse the court can consider in determining whether to issue a domestic
violenceorderofprotection.
II. Enables the department of justice's domestic violence fatality review committee to review a case
evenifthereisactualorpotentialcivillitigation.
