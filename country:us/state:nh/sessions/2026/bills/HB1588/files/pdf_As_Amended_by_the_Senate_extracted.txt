METHODOLOGY:
The bill may result in a possible indeterminable increase in municipal expenditures. The bill
indicates that any owner of commercially-zoned property who is improperly denied an
application for multi-family housing is entitled to reimbursement of reasonable attorney’s fees if
legal action is required to exercise the property rights established in the RSA 674:80. Because
the number of improper denials and amount of reimbursable legal costs cannot be predicted, the
fiscalimpactonmunicipalitiesisindeterminable.
