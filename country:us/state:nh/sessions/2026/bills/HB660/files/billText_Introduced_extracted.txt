The Lottery Commission states the language of this bill conflicts with that of RSA 284:23, I(d); specifically, both require HHR revenue (after breakage and prizes paid) to be distributed in different ways. For the purposes of this fiscal note, it is assumed that the intent of the legislation is for 10% of total HHR revenue be collected and paid to municipalities and the remaining HHR revenue to be distributed as otherwise described in RSA 284:23, I(d). In effect, this bill would distribute total HHR revenue as follows: 10% of total HHR revenue would be distributed to the municipality where the licensee operates HHRThe remaining 90% of total HHR revenue would be distributed (per RSA 284:22-b) as follows: 75% (equivalent to 67.5% of total HHR revenue) to the game operator16.25% (equivalent to 14.625% of total HHR revenue) to the Lottery Commission8.75% (equivalent to 7.875% of total HHR revenue) to charities In FY 2024, total revenue for HHR was $143,381,237.
