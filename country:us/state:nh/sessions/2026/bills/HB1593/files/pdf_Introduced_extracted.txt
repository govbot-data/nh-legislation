ANALYSIS
This bill codifies the language of section 504 of the Rehabilitation Act of 1973, which prohibits
discriminationbasedondisabilitybyrecipientsandprovidersofstateorfederalfinancialassistance.
The bill also codifies remedies available under section 505, which includes reasonable attorneys' fees
forcertainprevailingparties.

systems, which could affect prosecution, incarceration, probation, and parole costs, for the state,
as well as county and local governments. A summary of such costs can be found at:
