ANACT relative to the disclosure of rights and responsibilities during the sale of a mobile
homeinamobilehomepark.

COMMITTEE: Housing
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ANALYSIS
This bill clarifies repair notice timing and responsibilities, requires disclosure of purchaser
approval criteria, mandates seller communication access, and reduces park fees after repeated
denialsofqualifiedbuyersinlargermobilehomeparks.
