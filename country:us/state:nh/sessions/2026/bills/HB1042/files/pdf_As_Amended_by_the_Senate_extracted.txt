AMENDEDANALYSIS
This bill raises theunified contingent credit limit applicabletoNew Hampshirebusiness finance
authority projects and programs. The bill also raises the cap on outstanding obligations for which
thehousingfinance authorityis allowed and increases thebonding authorityfor thehousing finance
authority.
