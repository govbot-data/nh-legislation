ANALYSIS
This bill allows landlords to require a security deposit of up to 2 months’ rent from applicants
who do not meet specified approval criteria, establishes disclosure and re-screening requirements,
andclarifiesconditionsunderwhichthehigherdepositmayberemovedorrefunded.
