BeitEnactedbytheSenateandHouseofRepresentativesinGeneralCourtconvened:
1 1 New Section; Participation in Federal Immigration Enforcement; Requirements for Law
2 EnforcementOfficers. AmendRSA106-Pbyinsertingaftersection1thefollowingnewsection:
3 106-P:2 RequirementforLawEnforcementOfficers.
4 I. Any state, county, or local law enforcement officer that is participating in the federal
5 287(g) program as outlined in RSA 106-P:1 shall be required to identify themselves by name, badge
6 number, and agency and shall state that they are operating under the authority of United States
7 Immigration and Customs Enforcement while they are operating under the authority of the 287(g)
8 agreement.
9 II. Any state, county, or local law enforcement officer that is participating in the federal
10 287(g) program as outlined in RSA 106-P:1 shall remain unmasked while they are operating under
11 theauthorityofthe287(g)agreement.
12 2 EffectiveDate. Thisactshalltakeeffect60daysafteritspassage.
