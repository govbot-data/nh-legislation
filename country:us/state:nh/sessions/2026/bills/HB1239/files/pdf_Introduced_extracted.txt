ANALYSIS
This bill prohibits certain registered offenders who are required to register due to a sexual
offense against a child under 13 years of age from appearing within 1,000 feet of certain locations
undercircumstancesthatwouldwarrantalarm.
Thisbillisarequestofthedepartmentofsafety.
