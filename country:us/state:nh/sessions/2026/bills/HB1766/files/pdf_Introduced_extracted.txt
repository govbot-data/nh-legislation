ANALYSIS
This bill prohibits the confiscation of animals from persons suspected of abuse of animals unless
suchpersonischargedwithcrueltytoanimalsortheanimalsrequiretemporaryprotectivecustody.
This bill also requires the state veterinarian or their designee to accompany an investigating
officer when livestock are the subject of a cruelty case in order to set probable cause criteria for the
takingoftheanimal.
