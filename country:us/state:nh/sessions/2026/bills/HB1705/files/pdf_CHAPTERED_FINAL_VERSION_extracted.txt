AMENDEDANALYSIS
This bill establishes a fund to cover the costs of enrolling and supporting first responders from small
communities into the state-provided employee assistance program (EAP) and outlines eligibility,
enrollment,andreportingrequirementsforthedepartmentofhealthandhumanservices. Implementation
oftheprogramiscontingentuponreceiptoffunding.
