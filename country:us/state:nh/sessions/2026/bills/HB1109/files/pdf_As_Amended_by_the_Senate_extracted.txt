AMENDEDANALYSIS
Thisbill:
I. Requires a public employer to provide written noticeto a public employeeof the cause of their
suspension and its anticipated duration, if known, requires periodic updates to the public employee
until a final decision is made, and exempts public employees covered by collective bargaining
agreementsthatestablishdisciplinaryproceduresfromthenoticerequirement.
II. Transfers certain positions in the department of corrections to group II retirement system
status.
