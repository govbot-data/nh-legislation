FISCALIMPACT:
This bill modifies the terminology and purpose relative an adequate education and adds
additional references to the statute defining the content of an adequate education to the
adequacy calculation formula. This bill does not change the grant amounts awarded to districts,
charter schools, or education freedom accounts pursuant to RSA 198:40-a, nor does it modify the
content of an adequate education pursuant to RSA 193-E:3-b, I(a). Therefore, the proposed bill
willhavenofiscalimpact.
