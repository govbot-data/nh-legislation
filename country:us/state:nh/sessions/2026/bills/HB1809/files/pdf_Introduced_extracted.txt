METHODOLOGY:
This bill authorizes the medical use of psilocybin in the state, pending a determination by the
newly-established medical psilocybin advisory board that a regulatory program is ready for
implementation. If the board does not make such a determination within two years of the bill
becoming law, the remainder of the bill will not take effect. Assuming the program becomes
operational,theDepartmentofHealthandHumanServiceswillberequiredto:
1. Establishandoverseetheprogram;
2. Approve health care providers, and provider/producers, for the cultivation and
administration of medical psilocybin to qualified patients, and maintain a list of
approvedproviders/producers;
3. Collectdataforprogramevaluationandtheuseofbestpractices;and
4. Adopt rules for the proper administration and regulation of the program, including on
eligibility,applicationprocedures,andqualifications.
The Department would also be responsible for providing administrative support to the advisory
board. Assuming the program ultimately goes into effect, the Department anticipates needing
one new administrator and one new program administrator. Including equipment and other
upfront expenses, the Department anticipates costs of $250,000 in the first full year of
implementation,followedby$224,000and$236,000inthesecondandthirdyears,respectively.
