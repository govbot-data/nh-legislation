BeitEnactedbytheSenateandHouseofRepresentativesinGeneralCourtconvened:
1 1 ShortTitle. Thisactshallbeknownas“Grace’sLaw”.
2 2 New Section; Services for the Developmentally Disabled; Right to Preferred Method of
3 Communication. AmendRSA171-Abyinsertingaftersection4thefollowingnewsection:
4 171-A:4-a RighttoPreferredMethodofCommunication.
5 I. An individual with a communication disability who is receiving services under this
6 chapter shall have the right to select and utilize their preferred method of communication that
7 enables such individual to independently express their own thoughts and wishes. Methods of
8 communication that may enable an individual to independently express themselves include, but are
9 notlimitedto,anyofthefollowingmethods:
10 (a) AugmentativeandAlternativeCommunication(AAC)techniquesanddevices.
11 (b) Independentuseofletterboardsandindependenttyping-basedcommunication.
12 (c) Signlanguageandnon-verbalgesturalsystems.
13 (d) Speech-generatingdevicesoranyotherassistivetechnology.
14 II. Every school, day, residential and other program or service provider involved in the
15 implementation of the individual’s individualized education program or the individual’s service
16 agreement shall make all reasonable efforts to accommodate an individual’s preferred method of
17 communication, including securing necessary training to communicate with the individual using the
18 individual’spreferredmethodofcommunication.
19 3 EffectiveDate. Thisactshalltakeeffect60daysafteritspassage.
