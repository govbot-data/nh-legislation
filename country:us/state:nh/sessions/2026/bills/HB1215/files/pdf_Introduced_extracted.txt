4 171-A:4-a RighttoPreferredMethodofCommunication.
5 I. Anindividualwithadevelopmental disabilitywhoisreceivingservicesunderthis chapter

8 deliverysystem:
9 (a) AugmentativeandAlternativeCommunication(AAC)techniquesanddevices.
10 (b) Independentuseofletterboardsandindependenttyping-basedcommunication.
11 (c) Signlanguageandnon-verbalgesturalsystems.
12 (d) Speech-generatingdevicesoranyotherassistivetechnology.
13 II. Trained staff, including direct support professionals (DSPs), educators, and health care
14 providers of communication methods shall make all reasonable efforts to accommodate an
15 individual’spreferredmethodofcommunication.
