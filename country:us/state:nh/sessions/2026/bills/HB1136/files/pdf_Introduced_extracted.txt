ANALYSIS
Thisbill:
I. Allows municipalities to authorize accessory dwelling units by matter of right, conditional use
permit,orspecialexception.
II. PermitsmunicipalitiestoallowmorethanoneADUpersingle-familydwelling.
III. Clarifies that if a zoningordinance contains no ADU provisions, one ADU shall bedeemed a
permitted accessory use without municipal conditions other than building permits, if required by
municipalordinance.
IV. Expands ingress and egress standards to apply to all ADUs, not just attached units, and
prohibitsmunicipalitiesfromlimitingthechoiceofaccessmethod.
V. Allows municipalities to enforce local septic pumping ordinances while maintaining the
prohibitiononrequiringseparatesystemsforprincipalandaccessoryunits.
VI. Affirms municipal authority to require owner occupancy of one unit, without specifying
whichunit,andtoestablishreasonableenforcementregulations.
VII. Distinguishes between attached and detached ADUs for purposes of aesthetic regulation
andsizelimits;attachedADUsmayberegulatedforaestheticcontinuitywiththeprincipaldwelling;
detached ADUs may be subject to aesthetic standards only if such standards apply to the principal
dwelling;andmunicipalitiesmaysetminimumandmaximumsizelimits,butmaynotrestrictADUs
tolessthan750squarefeet.
VIII. Allows municipalities to impose a familial relationship between occupants of the ADU and
theprincipaldwelling.
IX. Allows municipalities to prohibit ADUs associated with townhouses, manufactured housing
orrented/leasedland.
X. Prohibits condominium conveyance of an ADU separate from the principal dwelling unless
expresslyallowedbythemunicipality.
