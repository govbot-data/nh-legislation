METHODOLOGY:
This bill prohibits non-conforming vehicles from stopping, standing, or parking in a parking
space properly designated for an electric vehicle, moped, or motorcycle, and establishes a
minimum fine of $62, with 20% of the fine going to the local municipality where the violation
occurred, and the remaining amount assumed to be credited to the state highway fund. The
Department of Safety states it would incur a one-time system change cost of $116,000 to
implement the provisions of this bill. As the number of violations cannot be predicted, this bill’s
revenueimpactonthestatehighwayfundandlocalgovernmentscannotbeestimated.
