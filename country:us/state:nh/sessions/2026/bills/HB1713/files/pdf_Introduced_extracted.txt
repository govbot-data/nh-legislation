ANALYSIS
This bill revises RSA 204-D to define eligible housing and income thresholds, authorize the
transfer of surplus state-owned property to the New Hampshire housing finance authority for
housing development, establish procedures for property disposition and affordability restrictions,
andrequirerulemakingtogovernoccupancy,resale,andmixed-incomehousingstandards.
