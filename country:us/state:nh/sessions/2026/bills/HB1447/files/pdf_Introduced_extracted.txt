ANALYSIS
Thisbill:
I. Definestheterm"sex"withinstatutoryconstruction.
II. Requires the state of New Hampshire and all political subdivisions of the state to adopt
policies that classify the use of restrooms, locker rooms, changing areas, and sleeping quarters by
sex.
III. Authorizes private entities to adopt the same policies relative to biological sex, and
establishes that all public or private policies adopted pursuant to the section shall not constitute
discrimination.
