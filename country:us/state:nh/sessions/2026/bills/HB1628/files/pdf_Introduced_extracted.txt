HOUSEBILL
ANACT requiring schools to provide excused absences for certain religious instruction,
defining released time courses, and permitting schools to adopt policies and
providecreditforreleasedtimecourses.
SPONSORS: Rep.W.MacDonald,Rock.16
COMMITTEE: EducationPolicyandAdministration
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ANALYSIS
This bill requires schools to provide excused absences for certain religious instruction, defining
released time courses, and permitting schools to adopt policies and provide credit for released time
courses.
