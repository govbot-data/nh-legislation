AMENDEDANALYSIS
Thisbill:
I. Establishes the process for issuing, extending, and appealingno trespass orders for municipal
andschoolproperties.
II. Establishes procedural safeguards for individuals affected by these orders, including the
righttoahearingandappeal.
III. Requires all applicable existing NTOs to be reviewed by the governing body and either
reissuedorrescindedwithin90days,orthoseNTOsnotacteduponbecomevoid.
