AMENDEDANALYSIS
This bill establishes maximum lot sizes for single-family residential uses based on the type of
infrastructure available to the lot. It requires municipalities to place a question on the ballot within
one years of the bill’s implementation to determine whether to rescind these provisions. If the ballot
measuresucceeds,themaximumlotsizeprovisionswillnotapplywithinthatmunicipality.
