─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ANALYSIS
This bill requires employers to notify public employees within 5 business days of receiving a
complaint against them, including the complaint text and relevant information, unless disclosure is
restricted by law, confidentiality, safety concerns, or investigative integrity. This bill also authorizes
thedepartmentoflabortoenforcetherequirementandimposecivilfinesfornoncompliance.
