ANALYSIS
This bill modifies the structure, membership, duties, and administration of the legislative youth
advisorycouncil. Thebillalsoestablishesbothanapplicationprocessforprospectiveyouthmembers
of the council and a penalty for violations of the chapter relative to the legislative youth advisory
council.
