─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ANALYSIS
This bill shortens the minimum excavation notice period from 72 to 48 hours, excluding
weekends and 6 holidays, and extends the validity of excavation notifications from 30 days to one
yearifsiteconditionsremainunchanged.
