BeitEnactedbytheSenateandHouseofRepresentativesinGeneralCourtconvened:
1 258:1 Findings and Statement of Purpose. The general court finds that the New Hampshire
2 residential home building industry is essential to New Hampshire’s economic vitality, workforce
3 development, and housing availability. The general court also finds that while the overwhelming majority
4 of New Hampshire builders operate with integrity and professionalism, isolated instances of fraud and
5 abuse, often from out of state, have caused harm to consumers and undermined confidence in the
6 industry. The general court recognizes that leaders within the New Hampshire residential building
7 community have expressed a commitment to strengthening accountability measures and supporting
8 reasonable mechanisms to promote transparency and consumer protection without creating unnecessary
9 financialorregulatoryburdens.
10 258:2 Committee Established. There is established a committee to study the implementation of a
11 statewide registration system for residential builders and contractors. For the purposes of this act,
12 “residential builder” shall mean a person or business entity whose primary source of income is derived
13 from the construction, reconstruction, or substantial renovation of residential properties consisting of 4 or
14 fewerdwellingunitsinasinglebuilding.
15 258:3 MembershipandCompensation.

22 258:4 Duties. Thecommitteeshall:
23 I. Examine the viability, process, and necessity of implementing a statewide registration for
24 residentialbuilders.
25 II. Assess whether a registration system would enhance consumer protection and address
26 instancesoffraud,misrepresentation,exploitation,andmiscommunication.
27 III. Evaluate the scope and structure of a registration system, including eligibility requirements,
28 renewalstandards,complaintprocedures,feestructures,oversight,anddisciplinarymechanisms.
29 IV. Identify which, if any, existing state agency or department would be best suited to oversee
30 and administer a registration system, including but not limited to the department of labor, the office of
31 professionallicensureandcertification,orthedepartmentofjustice.
