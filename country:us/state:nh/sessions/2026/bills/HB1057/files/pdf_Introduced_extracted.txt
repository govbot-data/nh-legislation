ANALYSIS
Thisbill:
I. Establishes default procedures for cost-sharing, maintenance, and dispute resolution among
landownerswhoholdcommonownershipofprivateroads.
II. Defines terms, clarifies the responsibilities of owners for maintaining roads that provide
actual and reasonable access, and outlines notice requirements, emergency actions, and enforcement
mechanisms.
III. Excludes roads governed solely by easement, deeded access, or license, and provides a
frameworkforequitablecostallocationandjudicialreliefintheabsenceofagoverningagreement.
