Thisbill:
I. Allows the commissioner of the department of education to initiate and implement a district
recovery plan if a school district's financial and operational condition is deemed a financial emergency by
thestateboardofeducation.

administratorcandidatesandmonitoringtheworkofbusinessadministrators.
