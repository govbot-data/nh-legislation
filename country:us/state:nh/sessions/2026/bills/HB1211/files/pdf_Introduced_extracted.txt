ANALYSIS
This bill states that reviews of state agency rules and guidance shall not defer to the agency's
interpretations but make any necessary interpretations de novo, and shall resolve doubts in favor of
limitingagencyaction.

3 541-A:24-a StandardsforReview.
4 I. In interpreting a state statute, regulation, or other subregulatory agency document, a
5 state court or an officer hearing an administrative action may not defer to a state agency’s
6 interpretationofit,andmustinsteadinterpretitsmeaningandeffectdenovo.
7 II. In actions brought by or against state agencies, after applying all customary tools of
8 interpretation, the court or hearing officer shall exercise any remaining doubt in favor of a
9 reasonableinterpretationthatlimitsagencypowerandmaximizesindividualliberty.
