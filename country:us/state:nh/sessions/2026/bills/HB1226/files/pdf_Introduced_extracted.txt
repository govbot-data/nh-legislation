ANACT relative to restricting the issuance of identification documents by unauthorized
individuals.
SPONSORS: Rep. Comtois, Belk. 7; Rep. Bailey, Straf. 2; Rep. Belcher, Carr. 4; Rep. DeRoy,
Straf. 3; Rep. Potenza, Straf. 19; Rep. Sirois, Hills. 32; Rep. Turcotte, Straf. 4;
Rep.Verville,Rock.2
COMMITTEE: Transportation
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ANALYSIS
This bill removes the ability of the director of the division of motor vehicles to manufacture,
advertise for sale, sell, or possess any fictitious, facsimile or simulated license to drive a motor
vehicle.
