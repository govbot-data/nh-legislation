METHODOLOGY:
This bill authorizes a special veterans license plate and establishes new entities, programs, and
dedicated funds. Sections of this bill will be discussed separately below, with fiscal impact
informationattheend:
Sections1and2
These sections add “continually appropriated” in two areas, related to the Conservation Number
Plate Fund. This would permit available funds to be expended, without necessitating spending
authority in the state operating budget (or any other legislation. These sections have no direct
fiscalimpact.
Section3
This section establishes the New Hampshire First for Veterans specialty license plate program
and creates the New Hampshire First for Veterans Number Plate Fund. The Department of
Safety is authorized to issue specialty plates, including vanity plates and additional motorcycle

Section4
This section establishes the New Hampshire First for Veterans Program and creates the New
Hampshire First for Veterans Authority as a public instrumentality of the state organized as a
nonprofit corporation. The authority is governed by a board of directors and is granted broad
powers to administer program funds, enter into contracts, award grants and loans, hire staff,
and accept public and private funding. The program is intended to support initiatives related to
veteran housing stability, employment, workforce training, health care access, education, and
otherservicesdesignedtoattractandretainveteransinthestate.
The section also establishes a dedicated fund, as well as a separate administrative fund, to
support operating expenses of the authority, subject to an annual revenue cap. Financial
assistance may be provided to municipalities and qualifying nonprofit organizations, with up to
50 percent of assistance authorized in the form of loans. The authority is required to conduct
annualindependentauditsandsubmitdetailedreportsonprogramactivitiesandexpenditures.
Since the program funding is expected to be derived from specialty plate revenues, donations,
federal funds, and other non-general fund sources, any fiscal impact to the state will depend on
the level of participation and revenue generation. Additional administrative costs may be
incurred if the authority hires staff or expands program operations; however, these costs are
intended to be supported by dedicated program revenues rather than general fund
appropriations.

PotentialFiscalImpacts
To implement the new plate type, the Division of Motor Vehicles states it would incur annual
costs of approximately $50,000 beginning in FY 2027, based on a $5 per plate cost and an
estimated 5,000 plate sets (10,000 plates) distributed per year. At a fee of $30 per set, the
Departmentestimatesrevenueof$150,000peryear.
The Department of Military Affairs and Veterans Services states it would need one
accountant/auditor position to implement this bill, at a cost of $105,000 in FY 2027, $106,000 in
FY2028, and$112,000inFY 2029. The Departmentassumes thepositionwouldbefunded with
generalfunds.
ThisbillmayalsohaveanindeterminableimpactontheDepartmentofAdministrativeServices,
Department of Business and Economic Affairs, and New Hampshire Employment Security, to
theextentprogramfundsareavailableandallocatedtoeachrespectiveagency.
The bill does not impose any mandates or direct costs on county or local governments. However,
municipalities and other political subdivisions could experience an indeterminable increase in
revenue or expenditures, to the extent they apply for and receive grants or loans under the New
HampshireFirstforVeteransProgram.
