ANACT criminalizing the use of small unmanned aircraft systems for the purposes of

BeitEnactedbytheSenateandHouseofRepresentativesinGeneralCourtconvened:
1 1 New Paragraphs; Unlawful Operation or Use of Small Unmanned Aircraft System. Amend
2 RSA644:23byinsertingafterparagraphVIIthefollowingnewparagraphs:
3 VIII. A person is guilty of a class A misdemeanor when the person knowingly uses a small
4 unmanned aircraft system to operate over any sporting event, concert, automobile race, festival, or
5 othereventwherepeoplearegathered,withoutpriorauthorization.
6 IX. A person is guilty of a class A misdemeanor when the person knowingly uses a small
7 unmanned aircraft system to operate over any critical infrastructure, without prior authorization.
8 For purposes of the this paragraph, "critical infrastructure" means petroleum refineries, petroleum
9 storagefacilities,chemicalstoragefacilities,chemicalmanufacturingfacilities,fuelstoragefacilities,
10 electric substations, power plants, electric generation facilities, military facilities, commercial port
11 and harbor facilities, rail yard facilities, drinking water treatment or storage facilities, government
12 buildings,andpublicsafetybuildingsorfacilities.
13 2 EffectiveDate. ThisactshalltakeeffectJanuary1,2027.
