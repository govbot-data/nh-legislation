AMENDEDANALYSIS
This bill updates requirements for home heating oil, kerosene, and propane dealers, adding
contract disclosures, limits on fees, prepaid-contract protections, delivery obligations, tank-removal
rules,propanerefundrequirements,andtreatingviolationsasunfairordeceptiveacts.
