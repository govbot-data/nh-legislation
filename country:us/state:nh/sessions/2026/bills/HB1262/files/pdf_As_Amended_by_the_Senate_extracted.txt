AMENDEDANALYSIS
Thisbillupdatesrequirementsforhomeheatingoil,kerosene,andpropanedealersbyexpanding
contract-disclosure rules, modifying prepaid-contract protections, setting requirements for tank
removal and refunds, limiting service-termination fees, and designating violations as unfair or
deceptiveacts.
