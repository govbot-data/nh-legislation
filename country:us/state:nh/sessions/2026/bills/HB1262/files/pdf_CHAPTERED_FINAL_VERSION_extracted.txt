AMENDEDANALYSIS
This bill updates requirements for home heating oil, kerosene, and propane dealers by expanding
contract-disclosure rules, modifying prepaid-contract protections, setting requirements for tank removal
andrefunds,limitingservice-terminationfees,anddesignatingviolationsasunfairordeceptiveacts.
