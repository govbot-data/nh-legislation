AMENDEDANALYSIS
Thisbill:
I. Clarifies that in case no declaration is filed by a candidate for any nomination to be voted for at a
primary, the chairperson of the appropriate party committee or their designee shall notify the secretary of
stateinwritingofapersontheydesignatetofillthevacancy.
II. Requires that a person designated to fill such a vacancy file a declaration of candidacy with the
secretaryofstatewithin10businessdaysfollowingthecloseofthecandidatefilingperiod.
