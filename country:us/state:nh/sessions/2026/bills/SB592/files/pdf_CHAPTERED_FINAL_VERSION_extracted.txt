AMENDEDANALYSIS
Thisbill:
I. Enables regional, conservation, and energy resource planning for habitat strongholds and wildlife
corridors.
II. Creates a commission to study transferring ownership of the Winnipesaukee River Basin program
toanalternativeauthority.
III. Creates the position of director of the Winnipesaukee River Basin program within the department
ofenvironmentalservices.
